# 04 — Conic Sections, Curves & Solids

*9 of 55 Qs (incl. 2 X). Cone sections + circle/polygon geometry.*

## 4.1 Conic Sections of a Cone — ⭐ asked 2× (+1 X)

| Cutting plane position | Section |
|------------------------|---------|
| Perpendicular to axis | Circle |
| **Inclined to axis, cuts ALL generators** | **ELLIPSE** ⭐ (Q12) |
| **Parallel to ONE generator** | **PARABOLA** (Q35 X — options were circle parts; fact re-askable) |
| Parallel to axis (or cuts both nappes) | Hyperbola |

- ⭐ **Eccentricity: e > 1 = HYPERBOLA** (Q17); e = 1 parabola; e < 1 ellipse. (e = distance from focus ÷ distance from directrix.)
- ⭐ **Major axis of ellipse = line through the FOCI terminated by the ellipse** (Q44).
- 🎯 Ellipse: sum of distances from two foci = constant = major axis.

## 4.2 Solids — ⭐ asked 3×

- ⭐ **Cone cut by plane inclined to base (not through base), apex portion removed = TRUNCATED cone** (Q37).
  - ⚠️ TRAP: frustum = cut PARALLEL to base; truncated = cut INCLINED. PSC keyed truncated because plane is inclined.
- ⭐ **Dodecahedron = 12 equal regular pentagons** (Q49). 🎯 Platonic set: tetra 4△, cube 6□, octa 8△, dodeca 12⬠, icosa 20△.
- ⭐ **Locus of point at fixed distance from fixed point = CIRCLE** (Q34).

## 4.3 Polygon Angles — ⭐ asked (+1 X)

- **Regular pentagon interior angle = 108°** (Q45 X — 108° absent from options; exterior = 72° was the bait ⚠️).
  - 🧮 Interior = (n−2)×180/n. Pentagon 108°, hexagon 120°, octagon 135°.
- ⭐ **Pentagon construction triangle: vertex angle 108°, base angles 36°** (Q55) (isosceles triangle on each side: 108+36+36=180).

## 4.4 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- "Section of cylinder cut inclined to axis" → ellipse.
- "Curve traced by point on circle rolling on straight line" → cycloid (on another circle: epi/hypocycloid; hypocycloid was an option in Q17).
- "Involute" → string unwound from circle; used in gear teeth.
- "Helix" → point moving around + along axis of cylinder.
- "Number of faces of octahedron" → 8.
- "Prism vs pyramid" → prism: two equal parallel end faces; pyramid: apex.
