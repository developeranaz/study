# 05 — Scales & Representative Fraction

*6 of 55 Qs. The only real numericals in ED — all one-line RF calculations.*

## 5.1 RF Basics — ⭐ asked 2×

- RF = drawing length ÷ actual length (SAME units).
- ⭐ **RF has NO unit** (Q39 — it's a ratio).
- ⭐ **RF > 1 → ENLARGING scale** (Q38); RF < 1 reducing; RF = 1 full size.

## 5.2 🧮 Worked PSC Numericals

1. 🔁2× (Q36, Q46 — verbatim repeat): room 5 m drawn as 25 mm →
   RF = 25 mm / 5000 mm = **1/200 (1:200)**. Free marks — same question twice.
2. Q8: scale 1:2, line measures 10 mm on drawing → actual = 10 × 2 = **20 mm**.
   ⚠️ TRAP: 1:2 means the drawing is SMALLER — multiply to get actual.
3. Q48: 10 cm on drawing represents 5 cm object → RF = 10/5 = **2:1** (enlarging).
   ⚠️ TRAP: read direction — drawing bigger than object → ratio > 1.

Template: RF = (drawn ÷ actual), convert to same units first; scale written drawn:actual.

## 5.3 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- "1 cm = 1 km on a map, RF = ?" → 1/100000.
- Types of scales: plain (units + tenths), diagonal (three units — units/tenths/hundredths), vernier, comparative, scale of chords (angles).
- "Scale used to measure three consecutive units" → diagonal scale.
- "Scale of chords is used to measure ___" → angles.
- Length of scale = RF × maximum length to measure.
