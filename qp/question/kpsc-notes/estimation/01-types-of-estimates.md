# 01 — Types of Estimates & Estimate Provisions

*13 of 65 Qs — biggest Estimation sub-topic.*

## 1.1 Types of Estimates

| Type | Basis | Note |
|------|-------|------|
| **Detailed estimate** | Item-wise quantities × rates | ⭐ **MOST RELIABLE/accurate** (Q38) |
| Preliminary/approximate/rough | Practical knowledge, for admin approval | for feasibility |
| **Plinth-area estimate** | plinth area × plinth-area rate | 🧮 Q29: 300 m² × ₹1500 = **₹4,50,000** |
| Cube-rate estimate | volume (plinth area × height) × cube rate | more accurate than plinth-rate 🎯 |
| Approximate quantity method | per running metre of wall/structure | 🧮 Q64: bridge 3 spans × 50 m × ₹30,000/RM = **3×50×30,000** |
| **Revised estimate** | Fresh detailed estimate | ⭐ needed when sanctioned estimate exceeded by **>5%** (Q39) — due to rates/price rise |
| Supplementary estimate | For ADDITIONAL works during progress 🎯 trap-pair with revised |
| Annual repair/maintenance estimate | 1–1.5% of building cost per year 🎯 |

## 1.2 Requirements & Documents — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Essentials of a good estimate: fully dimensioned drawings + detailed specifications + schedule of rates — ALL of the above** (Q31).
- ⭐ **REPORT gives a clear picture/idea of the whole project** (Q43) (part of estimate documents: report, specifications, drawings, abstract).
- Detailed estimate parts: measurement sheet (details of measurement) + abstract sheet (quantities × rates) 🎯.
- ⭐ Accuracy: areas measured correct to **0.01 m²** (Q40) (dimensions to 0.01 m; volumes 0.01 m³ 🎯).

## 1.3 The Percentage Provisions — ⭐ THE guaranteed marks (asked 5×)

| Provision | % of estimated cost | What it covers |
|-----------|--------------------|----------------|
| **Contingencies** | **3–5%** ⭐ (Q34) | Unforeseen/incidental expenses |
| **Work-charged establishment** | **1½–2%** ⭐🔁2× (Q33, Q42) | Site staff (mistris, chowkidars) charged directly to work |
| **Centage charges** | **10–15%** ⭐ (Q27) | Departmental supervision & establishment (when dept executes for others) |
| Tools & plants (T&P) | 1–1.5% 🎯 | |
| Electrification | 8% 🎯; water supply & sanitary: 8% 🎯 |

## 1.4 Rate Analysis — ⭐ asked

- Rate analysis = determining rate of an item from **materials + labour + T&P hire + overhead/supervision + contractor's profit** (Q41 was corrupt but this is the definition).
- ⭐ **Contractor's profit = 10%** (Q26) (on cost of materials + labour; overhead 2–5% 🎯).
- ⭐ **Out-turn (task) work = capacity/quantity of work per artisan per day** (Q60).
- Sundries, sundries in rate analysis; lump-sum (LS) items: provision without detailed measurement 🎯.
- ⭐ **Provisional sum:** amount for SPECIALIZED work by specialist firm, details unknown at estimate stage (Q30). ⚠️ trap-pair: **Prime cost (PC) sum** = amount for supply of MATERIALS/articles (fittings) to be provided at a fixed price 🎯.

## 1.5 PWD Procedure Neighbourhood 🎯 (Kerala PWD flavour — expect these)

- Administrative approval → technical sanction → budget allotment → tenders.
- Muster roll: attendance record of daily labour (asked under contracts in other banks 🎯).
- Schedule of rates (SoR): departmental rate book; current DSR.
- Measurement Book (MB): most important account record; measurements for payment.
- Securities: earnest money 2–2.5%, security deposit 10% (incl. EMD) — overlap with contracts questions 🎯.

## 1.6 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- "Estimate prepared for additional work" → supplementary (vs revised — price change on SAME work).
- Annual repair estimate % (1–1.5%).
- Cube-rate estimate contents (plinth area × height of building).
- Definition of abstract of estimate; order of estimate documents.
- Lump-sum vs provisional sum vs prime cost discrimination.
