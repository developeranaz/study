# 04 — Units & Modes of Measurement (IS 1200)

*8 of 65 Qs. Matching "item → unit" is free marks; also heavily asked in the Units & Measurements subject (53 Qs) — double payoff.*

## 4.1 The Item → Unit Master Table (⭐ = asked in this bank)

**Cubic metre (m³):** earthwork (excavation/filling), brickwork >10 cm thick, stone masonry, cement concrete (PCC/RCC), timber (wrought/put up).

**Square metre (m²):** plastering, pointing, painting/whitewash/colourwash/distemper, DPC, formwork/shuttering, flooring, roofing (tiles/AC sheet/GI), doors & windows (shutters), glazing, **brick wall ≤10 cm thick (partition)** ⭐ (Q12 — "measured in m² if thickness 10 cm").

**Running metre (m):** cornice, string course, fencing ⭐ (Q54 — **barbed wire fencing → metre**), skirting, pipes/rails, expansion joints, **painting of narrow widths ≤15 cm** ⭐ (Q18 — "painting up to 15 cm width or girth, not in conjunction with similar work → RUNNING metre").

**Kilogram / Quintal / Tonne:** steel reinforcement (kg or quintal), **bending & binding of steel → QUINTAL** ⭐ (Q17 — PSC key), structural steelwork (tonne/quintal), **stiff paint supply → kg** ⭐ (Q15).

**Number (Nos):** bricks supply, fittings, holdfasts, W.C. pans, steps.

(X-question topics: Q9 — *rubbing of cement/lime mortar surface* → **m²**; Q6 — brickwork (m³) + plastering (m²) quantities of one wall, garbled options; Q65 — NBC educational occupancy = Group B, see CT notes.)

**Litre:** ready-mixed paint supply, oils. ⚠️ trap-pair with stiff paint (kg)!

## 4.2 Measurement Rules — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **ISI/IS-1200 sequence of dimensions: LENGTH → BREADTH → HEIGHT (depth)** (Q59).
- ⭐ Areas correct to **0.01 m²** (Q40); dimensions to 0.01 m; cubic contents 0.01 m³ 🎯.
- ⭐ **Formwork: NO deduction for openings up to 0.4 m²** (Q23, IS 1200 Part 5).
- Plastering deduction rules 🎯 (near-certain future Q):
  - No deduction for openings ≤ 0.5 m² (and no addition for jambs).
  - Openings 0.5–3 m²: deduct ONE face only (jambs ignored).
  - > 3 m²: deduct both faces, measure jambs.
- Brickwork deductions 🎯: no deduction for openings ≤ 0.1 m² (lintel bearings, wall plates ends etc. per IS 1200 Part 3).
- Degree of accuracy in measurement: steel 1 kg; timber 0.002 m³ 🎯.

## 4.3 Mode-of-Measurement Logic (why)

- Thickness fixed/uniform → area (m²). E.g. DPC, plaster, slab formwork.
- All 3 dimensions vary → volume (m³).
- Cross-section uniform, length varies → running metre.
- Discrete articles → numbers.
This logic answers unseen items (e.g. "half-brick wall → m²" because thickness standard).

## 4.4 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- "Unit of measurement of DPC" → m²; "of cornice" → RM; "of steel reinforcement" → kg/quintal.
- "White washing measured in" → m²; "wood work in door frame" → m³; "shutters" → m².
- Deduction rule numericals (plaster with a 1 m² window: deduct one face).
- Lead/lift units for earthwork (see file 07).
