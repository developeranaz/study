## 8.1 Value Definitions — ⭐ TRAP CENTRAL

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Salvage value** | **Value at end of UTILITY PERIOD WITHOUT being dismantled** ⭐ (Q32) (can still be sold/re-used as-is) |
| **Scrap/junk value** | Value of DISMANTLED materials at end of life ≈ **10% of total construction cost** ⭐ (Q36) |
| Book value | Original cost − depreciation to date 🎯 |
| Market value | Price in open market at present (fluctuates) 🎯 |
| Assessed value | For tax purposes (municipal records) 🎯 |
| Distress value | Sale under pressure < market value 🎯 |
| Speculative value | Bought to sell later at profit 🎯 |
| Sentimental value | Above market due to attachment (ignored in valuation) 🎯 |
| Rateable value | Net annual letting value after deductions — for municipal tax 🎯 |

⚠️ Salvage vs scrap: **salvage = intact, scrap = dismantled (≈10%)**. PSC asked both — the other one is always the distractor.

## 8.2 Life & Depreciation

- ⭐ **Life of a building ≥ 50 years** (Q53) (RCC frames 50–75+; economic life vs physical life 🎯).
- Depreciation methods 🎯 (near-certain future set):
  1. **Straight line:** D = (C − S)/n per year.
  2. **Constant % (declining balance):** rate p = 1 − (S/C)^(1/n).
  3. **Sinking fund method** (depreciation + interest accumulates to replacement cost).
  4. Quantity survey method.
- Obsolescence = loss of value due to OUTDATED design/fashion/technology (≠ depreciation = wear & age) ⚠️ classic trap-pair 🎯.

## 8.3 Income-Based Concepts 🎯 (asked in Others/older papers — expect them)

- **Sinking fund:** annual sum set aside to accumulate replacement capital: I = (S·i)/((1+i)ⁿ −1).
- **Years' Purchase (YP)** = 100/rate% (in perpetuity) or 1/(i + sinking-fund coefficient) — Capitalized value = net annual income × YP.
- Annuity, capitalized value, gross vs net income, outgoings (repairs ~10–15% of gross rent, taxes, management, insurance…).
- Standard rent, economic rent.

## 8.5 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- "Value of building at end of life after dismantling" → scrap.
- Straight-line depreciation numerical: C=10 L, S=1 L, n=45 → D = 20,000/yr.
- YP numerical: rate 5% in perpetuity → YP = 20.
- "Loss of value due to change in fashion/design" → obsolescence.
- Rateable value definition (municipal tax base).
