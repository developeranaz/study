# 02 — Plinth Area, Carpet Area & Circulation

*10 of 65 Qs. Pure definitions + percentage recall.*

## 2.1 Area Definitions — ⭐ asked

| Area | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Plinth area | Built-up COVERED area at floor level (external dimensions, incl. walls) |
| **Carpet area** | **USABLE floor area EXCLUDING staircase, lift, walls, verandah, kitchen*, bath, WC** ⭐ (Q21) — "where you can lay a carpet" |
| Floor area | Plinth area − area of walls 🎯 |
| Circulation area | Passages, corridors (horizontal) + stairs, lifts (vertical) |

(*definitions vary; PSC's Q21 key = "usable floor area excluding staircase, lift, walls etc." → carpet area.)

## 2.2 The Percentage Table — ⭐ 5 questions from ONE table

| Quantity | % of plinth area | Source |
|----------|-----------------|--------|
| **Livable area (residential)** | **50–65%** ⭐ (Q35) |
| **Carpet area (office building)** | **60–75%** ⭐ (Q44) |
| **Vertical circulation** | **4–5%** ⭐ (Q37) |
| **Horizontal circulation** | **10–15%** ⭐ (Q46) |
| Walls | 10–15% 🎯 |

⚠️ CONTRADICTION (both are PSC keys — read the stem!):
- Q46: "Horizontal circulation area … % of the PLINTH AREA" → **10–15%**.
- Q50: "Area of horizontal circulation … of the TOTAL PLAN AREA of a residential building" → **20–25%**.
Rule: stem says *plinth area* → 10–15; stem says *total plan area* → 20–25 (PSC key D). If ambiguous, prefer 10–15%.

## 2.3 Approximate Estimate Numericals — 🧮 templates
