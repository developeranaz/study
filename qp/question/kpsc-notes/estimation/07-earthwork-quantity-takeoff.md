# 07 — Earthwork & Quantity Take-off Methods

*7 of 65 Qs. Lead/lift numbers + centre-line method + prismoidal comparison.*

## 7.1 Lead & Lift — ⭐ asked 2×

- **Lead** = HORIZONTAL distance of transporting excavated earth. **Standard lead = 30 m** ⭐ (Q48 — "earthwork in foundation trenches, drains: lead specified = 30 m").
- **Lift** = VERTICAL height of lifting earth. **Standard lift = 1.5 m** ⭐ (Q28 — "earthwork estimated for 30 m lead and 1.5 m lift").
- Extra rates for additional leads (per 30 m) and lifts (per 1.5 m) 🎯.
- ⭐ **Open-well excavation (dia 6 m): rate INCREASED 25% for the next 1.5 m depth after initial 1.5 m** (Q61 PSC key) — well/confined excavation costs more with depth.

## 7.2 Earthwork Volume — ⭐ asked

- 🧮 Q55: depth of cutting = GL − FL = 232.50 − 231.10 = **1.40 m** ✓ (then V = A×L with side slopes).
- Cross-section area with slopes s:1 → A = bd + sd²; V between sections by mean-area/trapezoidal/prismoidal 🎯.
- **Trapezoidal (end-area) vs Prismoidal:** ⭐ **prismoidal volume < trapezoidal** (Q51 — prismoidal is "small"er); prismoidal needs odd sections; prismoidal correction Cp = subtracted from trapezoidal result.
- Q49 (X) topic — prismoidal formula: **V = h/6 × (A₁ + 4Am + A₂)** for two end sections + middle 🎯 memorize; general: d/3[A₁+Aₙ+4Σeven+2Σodd].
- Borrow pit method (spot levels on grid) 🎯 likely at AE level.

## 7.3 Quantity Take-off: Centre-Line vs Long-&-Short-Wall — 🧮 asked

**Centre-line method:** total centre-line length × breadth × depth per layer.
🧮 Q25: room 6.5×5 m (internal), walls 30 cm → centre-line dims = (6.5+0.3)×(5+0.3) → CL length = 2(6.8+5.3) = **24.2 m** ✓
⚠️ TRAP: add ONE wall thickness to each internal dimension (centre to centre). If dims given are EXTERNAL, subtract one thickness.
- For cross-walls/junctions: deduct half breadth per junction from CL length 🎯 (T-junction rule: −b/2 each).

**Long-wall & short-wall (out-to-out/in-to-in) method:**
- Long wall length = CL length + one breadth (out-to-out); short wall = CL − one breadth (in-to-in); lengths change with each layer/footing step 🎯.
- Centre-line method faster, same result; best for symmetric footings without cross walls.

## 7.4 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- Earthwork numerical: trench 10 m × 1 m × 1.5 m = 15 m³ type.
- "Prismoidal correction is always subtractive" reworded.
- Long-wall length formula from centre-to-centre length.
- Extra lift/lead rate additions; initial lead/lift included in base rate.
- Filling in plinth/compaction %, shrinkage allowance 10% loose earth 🎯.
