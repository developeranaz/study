# 05 — Bar Bending Schedule & Steel Quantities

*7 of 65 Qs. Two formulas + one density = every question.*

## 5.1 Unit Weight of Bars — 🧮 GUARANTEED (asked 3×)

**THE formula: w = d²/162 kg/m** (d in mm) [exact: πd²/4 × 7850 × 10⁻⁶]

| d (mm) | kg/m |
|--------|------|
| **6** | **0.22** ⭐ (Q62) |
| 8 | 0.39 ⚠️ (distractor everywhere) |
| 10 | 0.62 |
| **12** | **0.89** ⭐ (Q57) |
| 16 | 1.58 |
| 20 | 2.47 |
| 25 | 3.85 |

- ⭐ **Density of steel = 7850 kg/m³ = 78.5 quintal/m³** (Q56).
- 🎯 Variation: "weight of 10 m of 8 mm bar" = 0.39×10 = 3.9 kg.

## 5.2 Cranked (Bent-up) Bars — ⭐ asked 3× (with a key conflict!)

For a 45° crank of depth d (vertical distance between top & bottom arms):

- **Extra length per crank = 0.42 d** — ⭐ Q47: total length of bar with two cranks = **L + 2(0.42D)** ✓
- **Excess length of ONE inclined side = 0.414 d** (√2·d − d = 0.414d… i.e. the inclined portion is √2·d/… ) — ⭐ Q58 key: **0.414 d** ✓
- ⚠️ Q52 PSC key said **0.45 d** for "additional length for one bent-up" — conflicting paper. **Rule: pick 0.42d when offered; 0.45d only if 0.42d absent.** (For 30°: 0.27d; 60°: 0.58d 🎯.)

Hook & bend allowances 🎯 (near-certain future Qs):
| Detail | Allowance |
|--------|-----------|
| Semi-circular hook (each end) | 9d (older books; IS 2502) |
| 90° bend | 8d? — standard: 4d–8d depending; common exam key: 90° bend = 8d, hook = 9d |
| Lap length (tension) | 50d common exam key (IS 456 development length based) |
| Cover: slab/beam/column/footing | 15/25/40/50 mm 🎯 |

## 5.4 Bar Bending Schedule (BBS) 🎯

- BBS columns: bar mark, diameter, shape, length of each bar, number, total length, weight.
- Cutting length of stirrup = 2(a+b) + 24d (with two 135° hooks; a,b = c/c stirrup dims) — exam-common key: +24d.
- Total bar length = clear span + 2×development/anchorage − concessions per bends.

## 5.5 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- Weight of 16 mm/20 mm bars (1.58/2.47).
- "Extra length for 60° crank" (0.58d).
- Hook length 9d question; lap 50d question.
- Number of bars from spacing: N = (length/spacing) + 1.
