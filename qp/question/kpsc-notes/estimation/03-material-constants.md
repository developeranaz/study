# 03 — Material Constants & Out-turns

*9 of 65 Qs. THE constants sheet of quantity surveying — every row is a potential question.*

## 3.1 Brick Constants — ⭐ asked 2×

- ⭐ **Standard (modular) bricks per m³ of brickwork = 500** — 🧮 Q5: wall 6×3×0.2 m = 3.6 m³ × 500 = **1800 bricks** ✓
- ⭐ **Traditional bricks per m³ = 495–500** — 🧮 Q11: 10 m³ → **4950** ✓ (PSC key uses 495/m³ for traditional).
- Rule: **bricks/m³ ≈ 500** for quick numericals; per m² of half-brick wall ≈ 50 🎯.
- Mortar in brickwork ≈ 25–30% of masonry volume (≈0.25–0.30 m³ per m³) 🎯 likely.

## 3.2 Concrete & Mortar Constants — ⭐ asked 3×

- ⭐ **Dry material for 1 m³ WET concrete = 1.52–1.54 m³** (Q7) (use 1.54; for mortar 1.25–1.33 🎯).
- 🧮 **Component volumes for nominal mix (total parts = a+b+c):**
  `Component = 1.54 × (part/total) per m³` — for 1:2:4 (total 7):
  - Cement: 1.54/7 = 0.22 m³ ≈ **6.4 bags** (bag = 0.0347 m³, 50 kg) 🎯
  - Sand: 0.44 m³; **Coarse aggregate: 0.88 m³** — ⭐ 🧮 Q8: per **100 m³ → 88 m³ CA** ✓
- ⭐ **Cement for 1:6 mortar = 5.5 bags/m³** (Q3) (dry factor 1.33: 1.33/7 = 0.19 m³ ≈ 5.5 bags).
  Ladder 🎯: 1:3 → ~10.8 bags; 1:4 → ~8.6; 1:5 → ~6.6; **1:6 → 5.5**; 1:8 → 4.2.
- Q14 (X) topic: cement per unit RCC 1:1.5:3 → 1.54/5.5 = 0.28 m³ ≈ 8 bags/m³ ≈ 400 kg/m³ 🎯.

## 3.5 🧮 Worked Templates

1. Bricks: volume of wall (L×H×t) × 500.
2. Cement bags in concrete: 1.54/(sum of parts) × 28.8 bags/m³-equivalent (1 m³ cement = 28.8 bags).
3. CA in 1:2:4: 0.88 m³ per m³ concrete.
4. Mortar volume in brickwork: 0.25–0.30 × masonry volume; cement bags = mortar volume × bags/m³ from ladder.

## 3.6 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- "Cement bags per m³ of 1:2:4 concrete" → ~6.4.
- "Water for 1 bag cement @ 0.5 w/c" → 25 L.
- "Number of bricks for 10 m³" (= 5000 standard).
- Sand in 1 m³ of 1:6 mortar (≈1.14 m³ wet? dry 1.33×6/7 = 1.14).
- Labour constants: mason+mazdoor gang composition for concrete.
