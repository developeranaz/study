## Style observations

- **Definition-recall dominates.** ~60% of Qs are one-line definitions (gauge, ballast,
  right of way, carriageway, gradient, turnout, hump yard). No derivations, almost no
  numericals — TE is a pure memory subject in this bank.
- **Railways ≈ Highways.** Unusual: railway engineering carries as much weight as
  highway geometrics. Don't skip rail terminology.
- **Exact-number recall:** flat-footed rail top width 66.67 mm, sleeper 275×25×13 cm,
  packing space 300–350 mm, roadway 12 m, shoulder 2.5 m, IRC 1934, ruling gradient 1 in 30.

## ⚠️ Trap catalogue (details in files)

- Overtaking zone: **minimum 3×OSD** (Q22); desirable = 5×OSD — both appear in textbooks.
- Ruling gradient 1 in 30 (plains) vs limiting 1 in 20 vs 1 in 20 for rolling — option
  sets mix all of them.
- Camber: CC 1 in 50–60 vs WBM 1 in 33–40 vs earthen 1 in 25–33 — same numbers reshuffled.
- Gauge measured between **inner faces** of rails (not centres).
- Pier (intermediate, permanent) vs abutment (end support) vs crib (temporary pier).
- Surface drain for heavy discharge: PSC key = **Rectangular** (Q29).
