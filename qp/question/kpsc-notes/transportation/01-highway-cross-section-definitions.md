# 01 — Highway Cross-Section & Definitions (9 Qs)

## Cross-section elements — the ladder (memorize as a picture)

From centre outward: **carriageway → shoulders → roadway (formation) → right of way**.

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q9):** Road **alignment** = position occupied by the **centre line** of the road (in plan).
- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q11, Q16 — asked 2×):** **Right of way** = the **area of land acquired
  for the road along its alignment**. (Q16 distractors: bottom width of excavation / top
  width of embankment / area occupied by vehicle — all wrong.)
- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q19):** **Carriageway** = portion of road surface used for **vehicular
  traffic only**. (Distractor "permanent way" = the RAILWAY track — classic cross-subject trap.)
- **Shoulder** = strip adjacent to carriageway for stopped vehicles / structural support.
- **Roadway (formation width)** = carriageway + shoulders.

## Institutions & history

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q18):** **Indian Roads Congress (IRC) set up in 1934** (on Jayakar
  Committee recommendation, 1927).
- 🎯 Likely variations: Jayakar Committee **1927**; Central Road Fund **1929**; Motor
  Vehicles Act 1939/1988; CRRI **1950**; Nagpur Road Plan **1943** (20-yr, road
  classification NH/SH/MDR/ODR/VR); Bombay plan 1961; Lucknow plan 1981.

## Traffic islands

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q21):** **Refuge island** = safety island serving the DUAL purpose of
  **protecting pedestrians AND segregating/channelizing traffic**.
- 🎯 Neighbourhood: traffic island = general raised area controlling movement; types =
  divisional, channelizing, rotary (rotary island at intersections), pedestrian refuge.

## Road drainage

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q29):** Surface drain shape most preferred for **heavy discharge** =
  **Rectangular** (PSC key as-is; V-shaped suits light discharge, semicircular is most
  hydraulically efficient section — don't overthink, follow the key).
- 🎯 Neighbourhood: side drains in cutting; catch-water drains on hill slopes; camber
  itself is the first drainage device (see file 02).
