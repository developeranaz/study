# 02 — Geometric Design: Camber, Gradient, Superelevation, Sight Distance (10 Qs)

## Camber (cross slope) — asked 2×

⭐ **PSC camber table (heavy rainfall → light rainfall):**

| Surface | Range | As % |
|---------|-------|------|
| **Cement concrete / high bituminous** | **1 in 50 to 1 in 60** (Q1, key A) | 2% – 1.7% |
| Thin bituminous | 1 in 40 to 1 in 50 | 2.5% – 2% |
| **Water Bound Macadam / gravel** | **1 in 33 to 1 in 40 = 3% to 2.5%** (Q12, key B) | 3% – 2.5% |
| Earthen road | 1 in 25 to 1 in 33 | 4% – 3% |

⚠️ TRAP: both Qs offer ALL four ranges as options — impervious surface ⇒ flatter camber.
Order: earth (steepest) → WBM → thin bit. → CC (flattest).

- Camber definition: convexity given to cross-section for drainage. Shapes: parabolic
  (preferred for fast traffic), straight-line, composite.

## Gradient — asked 2× + definition

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q20):** **Gradient** = rate of rise or fall of road surface **along its
  alignment**. (Camber = across; gradient = along.)
- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q14 & Q23, both keyed 1 in 30):** **Ruling gradient in plain terrain = 1 in 30** (IRC).

⭐ IRC gradient table:

| Terrain | Ruling | Limiting | Exceptional |
|---------|--------|----------|-------------|
| **Plain/rolling** | **1 in 30 (3.3%)** | 1 in 20 (5%) | 1 in 15 (6.7%) |
| Mountainous (>3000 m: steep) | 1 in 20 (5%) | 1 in 16.7 (6%) | 1 in 14.3 (7%) |
| Steep (up to 3000 m) | 1 in 16.7 (6%) | 1 in 14.3 (7%) | 1 in 12.5 (8%) |

⚠️ TRAP: Q14 options include 1 in 14.3, 1 in 12.5, 1 in 20 — all real values from other
rows of this table. "Ruling + plains" ⇒ always 1 in 30.
- 🎯 Neighbourhood: minimum gradient (drainage) ≈ 1 in 200 side drains; floating gradient;
  grade compensation on curves = 30+R/R %, max 75/R %.

## Superelevation

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q13):** For constant lateral friction coefficient, superelevation
  **increases with increase in speed and decrease in radius**. From e + f = v²/(gR):
  e ∝ v², e ∝ 1/R.
- (Q26, X): intent = "overturning on a curve is avoided by **superelevation/banking**".
- 🎯 Neighbourhood numbers: max SE = 7% (plain/rolling), 10% (hilly, no snow), 4% (urban);
  f = 0.15; design formula e = V²/225R (V in km/h, R in m, 75% speed, f = 0).

## Sight distance & driver reaction

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q24):** Stopping sight distance depends on **reaction time + braking
  time + speed of vehicle ⇒ ALL of these**. SSD = 0.278·V·t + V²/254f (t = 2.5 s).
- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q28):** **PIEV theory relates to the (total) REACTION TIME of the driver**.
  P-I-E-V = Perception, Intellection, Emotion, Volition.
  ⚠️ TRAP: option "Perception time" also offered — PIEV is the WHOLE reaction time, not
  just perception. Key = Reaction time (B).
- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q22):** Minimum length of overtaking zone = **3 × OSD**.
  ⚠️ TRAP: **desirable** length = **5 × OSD**. "Minimum" ⇒ 3×, "desirable" ⇒ 5×.
- 🎯 Neighbourhood: ISD = 2×SSD; OSD components d₁+d₂+d₃; on divided highways OSD not
  needed; headlight sight distance = SSD (valley curves).
