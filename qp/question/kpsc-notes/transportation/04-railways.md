# 04 — Railway Engineering (10 Qs — biggest TE sub-topic, tied with geometrics)

## Track components (bottom → top): formation → ballast → sleepers → rails

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q5):** **Ballast** = granular material spread on the formation for the
  **sleepers to rest upon**.
- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q6):** **Gauge** = clear horizontal distance between **INNER faces of
  the two rails at the top**. ⚠️ TRAP: not centre-to-centre.
- 🎯 Gauge values: BG = **1676 mm**, MG = 1000 mm, NG = 762 mm.

## Rails

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q30 & Q35 — asked 2×, verbatim):** Width of **top portion (head) of
  flat-footed rail = 66.67 mm** (Q30 key) / **66.68 mm** (Q35 key). Same fact; remember
  **≈ 66.7 mm** and pick whichever "66.6…" option appears.
- Rail types: double-headed, bull-headed, **flat-footed (FF, most common in India —
  needs no chair; fixed via bearing plates/spikes)**.
- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q3):** **Bearing plate** is used to fix **flat-footed rails on wooden
  sleepers** (distributes load, prevents sleeper damage). Fish plate + fish bolts join
  RAIL ENDS — ⚠️ classic distractor pair.
- 🎯 Neighbourhood: standard rail length BG = 13 m, MG = 12 m; rail designated by weight
  per metre (e.g. 52 kg/m); coning of wheels 1 in 20; tilt of rails inward 1 in 20;
  creep of rails.

## Sleepers

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q7):** Standard **BG wooden sleeper = 275 cm × 25 cm × 13 cm**
  (2.75 m × 25 cm × 13 cm). (MG: 180×20×11.5; NG: 150×18×11.5 — the wrong options.)
- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q8):** Minimum **packing space between two sleepers = 300–350 mm**
  (30–35 cm) — room for the beater/packing tool.
- 🎯 Neighbourhood: **sleeper density** = number of sleepers per rail length, expressed
  (n + x), e.g. 13 + 7 on BG; sleeper types: wooden, steel trough, CI pot, PSC (concrete —
  now standard, life ~50 yr).

## Points, crossings & yards

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q31):** **Turnout** = track from which the train **diverts** (complete
  assembly of points + crossing + lead rails taking track from one line to another).
- 🎯 Neighbourhood: points (switches) = tapered tongue rails; crossing (frog) = V where
  rails intersect; crossing number by right-angle/centre-line method.
- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q34):** **Hump yard** = marshalling yard where wagons are **pushed up
  to a summit by engine and gravitate down the slope** into sidings.
- 🎯 Yard types: flat yard (all shunting by engine), gravitation yard (natural slope),
  hump yard (artificial summit).

## Signals

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q32):** Shunting signal — **red band HORIZONTAL = STOP**; red band
  inclined (45°) = proceed. (Disc/ground signals for shunting moves.)
- 🎯 Neighbourhood: semaphore arm horizontal = stop/danger, lowered 45–60° = proceed;
  signal classification: stop/semaphore, warner, shunting, coloured light.

## Railway bridges (see also 05-bridges.md)

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q10):** Bridge mostly used for railway bridges of **SMALL spans** =
  **steel girder** (plate girder). Truss for larger spans; suspension unsuitable for rail.
