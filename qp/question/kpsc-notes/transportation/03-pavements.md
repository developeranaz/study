# 03 — Pavements & Bituminous Materials (3 Qs)

## Rigid vs flexible — asked 2×

- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q4):** The rigid pavement = **cement concrete pavement**.
  (WBM, bituminous, gravel = all flexible.)
- ⭐ **PSC FACT (Q27):** Pavement that **can resist tensile stresses** and has **very
  little resistance to deformation... = RIGID pavement** (key D). Rigid acts as a slab
  (beam action, tensile strength matters); flexible transfers load by grain-to-grain
  contact and tolerates deformation.

⭐ Comparison table (any row can become a question):

| Property | Flexible | Rigid |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Material | bituminous/WBM/gravel | cement concrete |
| Load transfer | grain-to-grain, layered | slab/beam action |
| Tensile strength | negligible | significant (design basis) |
| Design theory | layered system (CBR, IRC 37) | Westergaard (IRC 58) |
| Joints | none | expansion/contraction joints |
| Life | ~15 yr | ~30 yr |
| Reflects subgrade deformation | yes | no (bridges over weak spots) |

- 🎯 Neighbourhood: semi-rigid (lean concrete, soil-cement); pavement layers bottom→top:
  subgrade → sub-base → base → surface/wearing course; CBR test for flexible design;
  Westergaard's modulus of subgrade reaction k (plate bearing test).

## Bituminous materials (Q2 was X — corrupted options; learn the intent)

- ⭐ Intent of Q2: **softening point of paving bitumen grades ≈ 35 °C to 70 °C**
  (Ring & Ball apparatus).
- ⭐ Bitumen test table (🎯 highly likely future Qs):

| Test | Apparatus / unit | Typical value |
|------|------------------|---------------|
| Penetration | penetrometer, 100 g, 5 s, 25 °C; unit = 1/10 mm | 80/100 grade ⇒ pen 8–10 mm |
| Softening point | **Ring & Ball** | 35–70 °C |
| Ductility | briquette pulled at 27 °C | min ≈ 50–75 cm |
| Flash point | Pensky-Martens | min 175 °C |
| Viscosity grading | VG-10/VG-30/VG-40 | VG-30 common paving grade |

- 🎯 Neighbourhood: tar vs bitumen (bitumen = petroleum; tar = coal); cutback (fluxed
  with volatile oil — RC/MC/SC); emulsion (bitumen + water + emulsifier — RS/MS/SS);
  WBM uses stone aggregate + screenings + water (no binder).
