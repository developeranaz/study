# 02 — Hydrology, Precipitation & Rain Gauges

*13 of 64 Qs. Contains the single most-repeated question in the entire 800-question bank.*

## 2.1 THE Four-Time Question 🔁

⭐ **"Fall of moisture from the atmosphere to the earth surface in ANY form" = PRECIPITATION** — asked 4× (Q17, Q32, Q34, Q35), with distractors evaporation/transpiration/runoff/condensation. GUARANTEED mark.

## 2.2 Hydrologic Cycle Terms — ⭐ asked

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Evaporation | Water surface → vapour |
| **Transpiration** | **Loss of water from LEAVES of plants** ⭐ (Q59) |
| Evapotranspiration | Both combined = consumptive use 🎯 |
| Infiltration | Entry of water INTO soil surface ⚠️ |
| **Percolation** | **Water moving down through soil JOINING GROUNDWATER** ⭐ (Q16) |
| Runoff | Portion of precipitation flowing over surface to streams 🎯 (runoff = rainfall − losses) |
| Interception, depression storage | know names 🎯 |

## 2.3 Forms of Precipitation — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **SLEET = frozen RAIN drops (cooled to ice while falling through sub-freezing air)** (Q56).
- Glaze: rain freezes ON contact with cold ground ⚠; Hail: ice lumps >5 mm (thunderstorm updrafts); Snow: ice crystals; Drizzle: <0.5 mm, <1 mm/h; Rain: >0.5 mm 🎯.
- Types of precipitation by mechanism 🎯: cyclonic, convective, orographic (Kerala's monsoon on Western Ghats!), frontal.

## 2.4 Rainfall Measurement — ⭐ asked 2×

- ⭐ **Symon's rain gauge = NON-RECORDING type** 🔁2× (Q41, Q48) — standard IMD gauge (127 mm dia funnel; read daily 8:30 AM 🎯).
- Recording gauges: **tipping bucket** (electrical pulses), **weighing bucket**, **float (natural syphon — IMD standard recording)** — all three appear as distractors; know each mechanism 🎯.
- ⭐ **Rainfall cycle period in India = 35 years** (Q13 PSC key).
- Average rainfall over area 🎯: arithmetic mean, **Thiessen polygon**, isohyetal methods (isohyet = line of equal rainfall — pairs with isogonic/isoclinic traps!).
- Optimum number of rain gauges: N = (Cv/ε)² 🎯 AE-level.
- Index of wetness = actual/normal rainfall 🎯.

## 2.5 Graphs — ⭐ trap pair asked

- ⭐ **Hyetograph = graphical representation of RAINFALL (intensity) vs time** (Q23).
- ⭐ **Hydrograph = DISCHARGE vs time** (Q31 — "graphical representation of discharge and time").
- **Unit hydrograph** = hydrograph of direct runoff from 1 cm effective rainfall in unit duration ⚠️ distractor now, direct question soon 🎯 (assumptions: linearity, time invariance).
- Components of hydrograph: rising limb, peak, recession; base flow separation 🎯.
