## The Big Surprise

**~28% of "Irrigation" questions are actually FLUID MECHANICS** (Bernoulli, dimensionless numbers, manometers, notches, mouthpieces). KPSC files hydraulics under irrigation. Budget your study accordingly.

## Question Style

- Definition-recall dominates (~80%). Only 2 numericals in 64 Qs (duty formula, both from Δ=8.64B/D).
- **One definition asked FOUR times**: "fall of moisture from atmosphere in any form = PRECIPITATION" (Q17, 32, 34, 35 — near-identical). Free mark.
- Hydroelectric-project terms (penstock, surge tank, head/tail race) appear repeatedly — Kerala's hydel context (Idukki!) shows in the papers.
- Dimensionless numbers (Reynolds/Froude/Weber) = guaranteed one mark.

## Trap Catalogue

| Trap | Resolution |
|------|-----------|
| **Paleo vs Kor watering** | Paleo = FIRST watering BEFORE sowing ⭐; Kor = first watering AFTER sowing (deepest, most critical). |
| **Duty vs Delta vs Base period** | Duty D = area/unit discharge (ha/cumec) ⭐; Delta Δ = total depth of water (m); Base B = days. **Δ = 8.64B/D**. |
| Precipitation forms | Sleet = FROZEN RAIN drops ⭐; hail = ice lumps >5 mm from thunderstorms; glaze = rain freezing ON contact; snow = ice crystals. |
| Percolation vs infiltration | Infiltration = entry INTO soil surface; **percolation = movement DOWN to groundwater** ⭐ (Q16 keyed percolation for "joins underground water"). |
| Recording vs non-recording gauge | **Symon's = NON-recording** ⭐🔁2×; recording: tipping bucket, weighing, float. |
| Hyetograph vs hydrograph | Hyetograph = rainfall intensity vs time ⭐; hydrograph = DISCHARGE vs time ⭐ (both asked). |
| Weir vs barrage | Weir: raising water level by a solid obstruction (small/no gates) ⭐; barrage: gates over full length, control on crest. |
| Silt excluder vs extractor/ejector | Excluder = in RIVER pocket BEFORE head regulator ⭐; ejector/extractor = in CANAL after regulator. |
| Centre of pressure (vertical wall) | at **H/3 above BOTTOM** ⭐🔁2× (= 2H/3 below surface — read stem direction!). |
| Most efficient channel section | Overall best = **semicircular** ⭐; most economical RECTANGULAR: b = 2d ⭐; trapezoidal: half-hexagon. Match the stem's section type! |
| Froude/Reynolds/Weber/Mach | inertia ÷ **gravity / viscous / surface tension / elastic** forces. Mnemonic: "**F**alls **G**ravity, **R**uns **V**iscous, **W**ets **S**urface, **M**oves **E**lastic". |
