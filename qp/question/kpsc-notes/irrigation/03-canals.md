# 03 — Canals, Silt Theories & Channel Sections

*7 of 64 Qs (+ channel-section Qs shared with hydraulics).*

## 3.1 Canal Alignment — ⭐ asked

| Alignment | Feature |
|-----------|---------|
| Watershed/ridge canal | Along the ridge — irrigates BOTH sides, no cross-drainage works needed (ideal) 🎯 |
| Contour canal | Follows contour — irrigates ONE side only; needs CD works 🎯 |
| **Side-slope canal** | **At RIGHT ANGLES to the contours** ⭐ (Q2) — parallel to natural drainage, steep bed slope, no CD works |

Canal hierarchy 🎯: main canal (no direct irrigation) → branch (>5 cumec) → major distributary (0.25–5) → minor → watercourse/field channel. Q: "canal that does not directly irrigate" → main canal.

## 3.2 Channel Sections (efficiency) — ⭐ asked 2× ⚠️ read the stem!

- ⭐ **Most efficient cross-section overall = SEMICIRCULAR** (Q38) (max area for given perimeter).
- ⭐ **Most economical RECTANGULAR section: depth = HALF the width (b = 2d)** (Q49); R = d/2.
- Most economical TRAPEZOIDAL = half-hexagon (side slope 60°, i.e. 1:1/√3); R = d/2 🎯 near-certain.
- Practical channels use trapezoidal (semicircle impractical to build) 🎯.

## 3.3 Silt Theories — ⭐ asked

- **Kennedy's theory** 🎯: critical velocity ratio (CVR) m = V/V₀; V₀ = 0.55 m^0.64 (trial & error design; based on UP canals; silt supported by eddies from bed).
- **Lacey's regime theory**: true regime; silt factor f = 1.76√d(mm); V = (Qf²/140)^(1/6); P = 4.75√Q 🎯.
  - ⭐ **Shape of Lacey regime channel = SEMI-ELLIPSE** (Q58).
  - Lacey vs Kennedy differences (regime vs non-regime, eddies from whole perimeter) 🎯 classic.
- ⭐ **Silt excluder: placed in front of (upstream of) HEAD REGULATOR to remove silt** (Q21) — in the river pocket; **silt ejector/extractor = in the canal** downstream ⚠️ trap-pair.

## 3.4 Canal Structures — ⭐ asked

- **Divide wall** ⭐ (Q18 — all 3 statements TRUE): separates weir pocket, **prevents cross-currents/parallel flow**, provides **still pocket in front of head regulator**, serves as side wall of **fish ladder**.
- Cross-Drainage Works 🎯 (near-certain full question set):
  | CDW | Condition |
  |-----|-----------|
  | Aqueduct | Canal OVER drainage (canal FSL well above) |
  | Syphon aqueduct | Canal over drainage; drainage water syphons under pressure |
  | **Super passage** | **Drainage OVER canal; canal FSL LOWER than underside of drainage trough** ⭐ (Q4) |
  | Canal syphon | Drainage over canal; canal flows under pressure |
  | Level crossing | Both at same level (gated) |
  | Inlet & outlet | small drainage admitted into canal |
- Canal lining purposes: reduce seepage (main), increase velocity/duty, prevent weed 🎯; lined canal seepage loss lowest.
- Canal outlets/modules 🎯: non-modular, semi-module, rigid module (discharge independent of both levels).

## 3.5 🎯 High-Probability Variations
