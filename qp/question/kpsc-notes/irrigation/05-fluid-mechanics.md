# 05 — Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulics (filed under Irrigation by KPSC!)

*18 of 64 "Irrigation" Qs are hydraulics — the single biggest chunk. Pure definition/formula recall.*

## 5.1 Fluid Properties — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Ideal fluid = frictionless (non-viscous) AND incompressible** (Q25) (imaginary; no shear resistance).
- Real fluid: has viscosity. Newtonian fluid obeys Newton's law of viscosity (τ = μ·du/dy) 🎯.
- ⭐ **Poise = unit of (dynamic) VISCOSITY** (Q5) — CGS; SI: Pa·s = 10 poise; **1 poise = 0.1 Pa·s** (also in Units bank!). Kinematic viscosity ν = μ/ρ, unit **stoke** (cm²/s) ⚠️ trap-pair 🎯.
- ⭐ **Hydrodynamics = study of LIQUID in motion** (Q63) (hydrostatics = at rest).
- Surface tension (N/m), capillarity, compressibility, specific weight/mass/gravity 🎯.

## 5.2 Hydrostatics — ⭐ asked 3×

- ⭐ **Hydrostatic law: rate of increase of pressure VERTICALLY = weight density (γ) of fluid** (Q53) — dp/dz = γ ⇒ p = γh.
- ⭐🔁2× **Centre of pressure of water on a VERTICAL wall (depth H): at H/3 ABOVE THE BOTTOM** (Q46, Q50) — i.e., 2H/3 below surface. Total pressure P = γH²/2 per m length 🎯.
- ⚠️ Read direction in stem: "below free surface" → 2H/3; "above bottom/base" → H/3.
- **Piezometer measures PRESSURE** ⭐ (Q28) (moderate +ve pressures only; no vacuum).
- ⭐ **Differential manometer: DIFFERENCE of pressure between two points** (Q45) (simple manometer: pressure at a point; micromanometer: very low pressures 🎯).

## 5.3 Flow Kinematics & Energy — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Path line = path followed by a fluid PARTICLE in motion** (Q39) (streamline = tangent to velocity vectors at an instant; streak line = locus of particles through a fixed point ⚠️ trio-trap 🎯).
- Flow types 🎯: steady/unsteady (time), uniform/non-uniform (space), laminar/turbulent (Re), rotational/irrotational.
- ⭐ **Total energy of liquid particle = Pressure energy + Kinetic energy + Potential energy** (Q61) — Bernoulli: p/γ + v²/2g + z = constant (ideal, steady, incompressible, along streamline 🎯 assumptions likely).
- ⭐ **Vertical distance between Total Energy Line (TEL) and Hydraulic Gradient Line (HGL) = VELOCITY HEAD (v²/2g)** (Q1). HGL = pressure + datum head only; TEL always ≥ HGL, slopes down along flow 🎯.
- ⭐ **Pitot tube = velocity at a point in flowing stream** (Q29) (venturimeter/orifice meter = discharge in pipes ⚠️ distractors).

## 5.5 Orifices, Mouthpieces, Notches — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Nappe/vein = sheet of liquid flowing over a NOTCH** (Q40) (crest = notch bottom edge 🎯).
- Notch formulas 🎯: rectangular Q = (2/3)Cd·b·√(2g)·H^1.5; triangular Q = (8/15)Cd·tan(θ/2)√(2g)·H^2.5 (V-notch best for small discharge; asked in other banks).
- **Coefficients:** Cd = Cc × Cv; sharp-edged orifice Cd ≈ 0.62, Cv ≈ 0.97, Cc ≈ 0.64 🎯.
- ⭐ **External (cylindrical) mouthpiece Cd = 0.855** (Q62) (internal/Borda running full 0.707, running free 0.50 🎯 ladder likely).
- ⭐ **Critical depth relation: h = v²/g** (Q47 PSC key — at critical flow in rectangular channel, depth = v²/g; specific energy minimum, F = 1 🎯).

## 5.6 Pipe Flow Extras 🎯

- Darcy friction loss h_f = 4fLv²/(2gD); major vs minor losses; entry loss 0.5v²/2g, exit v²/2g.
- Power transmission efficiency (H−h_f)/H ⭐ (in dams file); max when h_f = H/3.
- Hydraulic jump: energy dissipator, forms when supercritical → subcritical 🎯.
- Water hammer (see surge tank) 🎯.
