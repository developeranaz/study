# 01 — Water Requirement of Crops, Duty–Delta & Irrigation Methods

*13 of 64 Qs (incl. wells).*

## 1.1 Duty, Delta, Base Period — 🧮 THE numerical zone

| Term | Definition | Unit |
|------|-----------|------|
| **Duty (D)** | **Irrigating capacity of a UNIT quantity of water** ⭐ (Q20) — area irrigated per cumec of continuous supply | ha/cumec |
| Delta (Δ) | Total DEPTH of water required by crop over base period | m / mm |
| Base period (B) | Days from first to last watering | days |
| Crop period | Sowing to harvesting (≥ base period) 🎯 |

**Master relation: Δ = 8.64 B / D** (Δ in m, B days, D ha/cumec)
- 🧮 Q33: Δ = 1500 mm = 1.5 m, B = 150 d → D = 8.64×150/1.5 = **864 ha/cumec** ✓ (Q36 corrupt asked the formula itself — memorize it!)
- Derivation feel: 1 cumec × 1 day = 8.64 ha·cm... (86,400 s × 1 m³ = 86,400 m³ = 8.64 ha·m per 100).

**Duty improves (increases) when** losses decrease: lined canal, nearer the field, well irrigation 🎯.
- ⭐ **Duty of tube well irrigation HIGHER than canal irrigation** (Q15) — minimal conveyance loss.
- Duty measurement points ⭐ (Q6 match, PSC key): head of MAIN canal → gross quantity; head of BRANCH canal → lateral quantity; OUTLET of canal → outlet factor; head of the FIELD → net quantity. (Duty increases toward the field as losses drop out.)
- **Flow duty vs quantity duty (tanks: ha/Mm³)** 🎯.
- ⭐ **Capacity factor = average discharge / full-supply discharge** at a point (Q7). Time factor = days canal ran / days of demand 🎯; outlet factor = duty at outlet 🎯.

## 1.2 Command Areas — ⭐ asked

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **GCA (Gross Command Area)** | **TOTAL area which CAN be irrigated by a canal system** ⭐ (Q10) (fit + unfit land) |
| CCA (Culturable Command Area) | GCA − unculturable (ponds, roads, villages) 🎯 |
| Q19 (X) topic | "total area economically irrigated without water-quantity limit" = **commanded area** definition family — know GCA/CCA/intensity |
| Intensity of irrigation | % of CCA irrigated per season/year 🎯 |

## 1.3 Waterings & Soil-Moisture Terms — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Paleo = FIRST watering BEFORE SOWING** (Q37) (to moisten soil for sowing).
- **Kor watering = first watering AFTER sowing** — maximum depth needed; **kor depth & kor period** 🎯.
  - ⭐ **Kor depth of SUGARCANE = 165 mm** (Q57 PSC key) (rice ≈ 190 mm, wheat ≈ 135 mm 🎯).
- ⭐ **Field capacity = water held in soil AFTER gravitational water drains** (Q51).
- Wilting point/coefficient = moisture at which plants wilt permanently 🎯; available moisture = field capacity − wilting point 🎯 near-certain.
- Saturation capacity, soil-moisture deficiency; consumptive use (evapotranspiration) = evaporation + transpiration 🎯.

## 1.4 Crop Seasons 🎯 (Kerala PSC staple)

- **Kharif**: monsoon sowing (Jun–Oct) — rice, maize, cotton. **Rabi**: winter (Oct–Mar) — wheat, gram. Kharif/Rabi ratio; Perennial (sugarcane ~12 months, B ≈ 320–360 d).
- Crop rotation purpose: soil fertility 🎯.

## 1.5 Methods of Irrigation — ⭐ asked

| Method | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Sprinkler** | **Water applied as SPRAY like rain** ⭐ (Q11) — undulating land, sandy soils, saves 30–50% water |
| Drip/trickle | Highest efficiency (~90%), row crops, water-scarce 🎯 |
| **Check flooding** | **Crops that can stand water for some time** ⭐ (Q22 PSC key) — levees divide field into checks |
| Free flooding, border strip, basin (orchards), furrow (row crops: potato/sugarcane) | know one-liners 🎯 |
| Subsurface | capillarity through underground pipes 🎯 |

## 1.6 Wells & Tube Wells — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Usual capacity of tube well = 0.056 cumec** (Q52) (≈200 m³/h, serves ~400 ha).
- Open well vs tube well; strainer/cavity/slotted types 🎯; well yield tests (recuperation) 🎯.
