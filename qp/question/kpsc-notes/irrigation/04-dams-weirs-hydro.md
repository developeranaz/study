## 4.1 Dams — ⭐ asked

| Dam | Key fact |
|-----|----------|
| **Gravity dam** | Resists by its own WEIGHT; ⭐ **constructed with MASONRY** (Q3) or concrete (rigid dam); e.g. Bhakra (concrete), Nagarjuna Sagar (masonry — world's largest) 🎯 |
| **Earthen dam** | ⭐ **Made of locally available soils & gravels** (Q12); types: homogeneous, zoned, diaphragm 🎯; failure: piping, overtopping (most common cause 🎯), sloughing |
| Rockfill | Rock + impervious membrane ⚠️ distractor |
| Arch dam | Transfers load to abutments (Idukki = arch dam! 🎯 near-certain Kerala question) |
| Buttress | Deck slab on buttresses 🎯 |

- Forces on gravity dam 🎯: self-weight, water pressure, **uplift**, wave/silt/ice/earthquake; failure modes: overturning, sliding, crushing, tension — elementary profile = right triangle, base b = H/√(G−c)… (AE-level).
- Spillway = safety valve of dam 🎯 (ogee most common; siphon, chute, shaft…).

## 4.2 Weirs & Barrages — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Weir = impervious barrier across river to RAISE WATER LEVEL** (Q24).
- Barrage: gates over whole width, crest low, better control ⚠️ trap-pair 🎯.
- Diversion headworks components 🎯: weir/barrage, undersluices (scour silt in pocket), **divide wall** ⭐ (canal file), fish ladder (fish migration — momentum!), head regulator, silt excluder, guide banks (Bell's bunds), marginal bunds.
- Seepage theories under weirs 🎯 (AE-level but cheap): Bligh's creep theory (L = CH), Lane's weighted creep, **Khosla's** (exit gradient — modern).

## 4.3 Hydroelectric Project Terms — ⭐ 6 questions!

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Gross head** | **Difference of water level between HEAD RACE and TAIL RACE** ⭐ (Q42) (net head = gross − friction losses 🎯) |
| Head race | Channel carrying water TO the turbine/powerhouse |
| **Tail race** | **Channel carrying water AWAY from powerhouse after turbines** ⭐ (Q64) |
| Penstock | Pressure pipe from forebay/reservoir to turbine 🎯 |
| **Surge tank** | ⭐🔁3× (Q26, Q43, Q54) — **guards against / reduces WATER-HAMMER pressure in penstock**; placed just upstream of powerhouse/on penstock to control pressure variations |
| Forebay | Balancing reservoir before penstock ⚠️ distractor |
| Draft tube | Reaction turbines' outlet recovery 🎯 |

- ⭐ **Efficiency of power transmission through pipe = (H − h_f)/H** (Q30) (max efficiency at h_f = H/3 → 66.7% 🎯 likely follow-up).
- Water hammer = pressure surge from sudden valve closure 🎯 (also asked as surge-tank purpose).
- Hydel classification: run-off river / storage / pumped storage 🎯.

## 4.4 Reservoir Bits 🎯
