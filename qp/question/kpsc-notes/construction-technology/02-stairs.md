# 02 — Stairs

*11 of 66 CT Qs — the single most-asked component. One terminology table + one numbers table = all marks.*

## 2.1 Terminology — ⭐ every row has been asked or will be

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Tread | Horizontal top of a step (the part you step on) |
| Riser | Vertical face between treads |
| **Flight** | **Series of steps between landings** ⭐ (Q18) |
| Landing | Level platform between flights |
| **String (stringer)** | **Inclined member supporting the ENDS of steps** ⭐ (Q36) |
| **Soffit** | **Under-surface of the stair** ⭐ (Q23) |
| **Pitch** | **Angle of inclination of stair with floor** ⭐ (Q35) |
| Nosing | Projecting edge of tread beyond riser |
| Scotia | Moulding under the nosing ⚠️ (appears as distractor twice) |
| Winder | Tapering step for changing direction ⭐ (avoid them! Q34) |
| Going | Horizontal distance between successive risers 🎯 |
| Baluster / Balustrade / Handrail | Vertical post / row of balusters + rail / top rail 🎯 |
| Newel post | Post at head/foot of stair carrying handrail 🎯 |
| Walking line | ~45 cm from centre of handrail 🎯 |
| Headroom | Clear vertical distance from tread nosing to ceiling above |

## 2.2 Types of Stairs — ⭐ asked

- Straight; **Quarter-turn** (90°); **HALF-TURN = turning through TWO right angles (180°)** ⭐ (Q17) — dog-legged (no well) & open-newel (with well); Three-quarter turn (270°); Geometrical (no newel, continuous handrail); Circular/spiral/helical; Bifurcated 🎯.
- ⭐ **Ramp = sloping surface connecting floors** (Q30) (gradient ≤ 1:12 for accessibility 🎯; escalator = moving stair, lift = vertical car).

## 2.3 Requirements of a Good Stair — ⭐ NUMBER TABLE (Q34 tested 4 at once!)

| Requirement | Value |
|-------------|-------|

| **Tread/going (residential, without nosing)** | **25 cm min** ⭐ (Q31) (public buildings 27–30 cm 🎯) |
| Riser (residential) | 15–19 cm; public 12–15 cm 🎯 |
| **Steps per flight** | **min 3** ⭐ (Q55), **max 12** ⭐ (in Q34) |

| Width (residential) | 90 cm–1.0 m; public ≥ 1.5 m 🎯 |

**Proportioning rules 🎯 (classic future numericals):**
- 2R + T = 60 cm (approx. rule)
- R × T ≈ 400–450 cm²
- R + T ≈ 40–45 cm

## 2.4 The Riser/Tread Count Numerical — 🧮 asked (Q16)

**Rules:** No. of risers = total height ÷ rise. **No. of treads = risers − 1 per flight** (top landing replaces last tread).

🧮 Q16: floor-to-floor 300 cm, rise 15 cm → risers = 300/15 = **20**, treads = **19** ✓ (dog-legged: e.g. 10+10 risers with mid-landing).

🎯 Variation to expect: given riser 15 cm & tread 25 cm and floor height, find number of steps per flight, or going length of flight = treads × tread width.

## 2.5 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- Dog-legged vs open-newel (well) distinction.
- Materials: RCC stairs most common (fire-resistant, any shape).
- "Stair located near entrance"; "width of landing ≥ width of stair" (already in Q34).
- Handrail height: 90 cm from nosing line 🎯.
- Spiral stair suits: limited space, secondary access.
