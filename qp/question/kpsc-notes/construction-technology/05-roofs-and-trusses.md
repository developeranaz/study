## 5.1 Pitched-Roof Terminology — ⭐ asked

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Ridge** | **Highest horizontal line where two opposite slopes meet** ⭐ (Q56) |
| Eaves | Lowest edge of roof (drips rainwater) ⚠️ distractor |
| Hip / valley | External / internal angle of intersecting slopes 🎯 |
| Purlin | Horizontal member over trusses supporting common rafters ⚠️ distractor |
| Common rafter / principal rafter | Inclined members: carry battens / main truss member 🎯 |
| Battens, boarding, cleats, template | know roles 🎯 |
| **Kneeler** | **Coping stone between apex and skew corbel of a GABLE wall** ⭐ (Q37) (apex stone = saddle/finial at top; skew corbel at base of gable coping) |
| Finial | Ornamental top at apex ⚠️ distractor in Q37 |
| Verge, barge board, gablet | gable-edge terms 🎯 |

## 5.2 Roof Types — ⭐ asked

- **Lean-to (pent/aisle):** slopes ONE direction — verandahs, sheds (span ≤ 2.4 m) 🎯.
- ⭐ **Gable roof: common sloping roof, slopes in TWO directions** (Q51).
- Hip roof: FOUR slopes (hipped ends) ⚠️ distractor; Gambrel (2 directions, break in slope), **Mansard (4 directions with break — two slopes each side)** 🎯, Deck roof (plane top + 4 slopes).
- ⭐ **Roof slope should NOT exceed 30° to minimize WIND forces** (Q59) (PSC key; also batten roofs min slope depends on covering: GI sheet 1 in 4, AC sheet, country tiles ~30–45° 🎯).

## 5.4 Truss Members — Q39 (corrupt) topic

- ⭐ Q39 intended: **inclined braces preventing sagging of principal rafters = STRUTS** (compression members from king/queen post to rafters).
- King-post truss parts: king post (vertical, tension), tie beam, principal rafters, struts 🎯.
- Queen-post truss adds: TWO queen posts, straining beam (between queen-post heads, compression), straining sill 🎯.
- Members in TENSION: tie beam, king/queen posts. COMPRESSION: rafters, struts, straining beam. ⚠️ classic trap: king post is a TENSION member despite being a "post". 🎯 near-certain.

## 5.5 Roof Coverings & Flat Roofs 🎯

- Coverings: thatch (cheapest), AC sheets, GI corrugated (lap: end 15 cm, side 2 corrugations), Mangalore tiles (⭐ Estimation bank: 16 tiles/m²), slates, red-clay pan tiles (Kerala!).
- Flat roofs: madras terrace, mud-phuska, RCC slab with weathering course — advantages/disadvantages vs pitched in heavy-rain Kerala (pitched preferred for rain).

## 5.6 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- "Roof sloping in four directions" → hip; "one direction" → lean-to.
- King-post span (5–8 m) — the missing row PSC will ask next.
- Camber provided in tie beam; ridge line member names.
- Purlin spacing, common-rafter spacing numbers (rarely asked at JE).
