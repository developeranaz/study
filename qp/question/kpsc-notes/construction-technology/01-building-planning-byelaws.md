# 01 — Building Planning & Bye-laws (NBC / KMBR) ⭐ BIGGEST CT TOPIC

*16 of 66 CT Qs. Almost pure number-recall. Kerala PSC loves this because Kerala JEs enforce KMBR daily.*

## 1.1 Principles of Building Planning — ⭐ definitions asked

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Orientation** ⭐ (Q62) | **Placing building units w.r.t. sun, prevailing wind, rain and topography** |
| Aspect | Arrangement of doors/windows to enjoy sun/breeze/scenery (kitchen→east, bedroom→SW etc.) 🎯 |
| Prospect | Views as seen FROM the windows (impression from outside) 🎯 ⚠️ trap-pair with aspect |
| **Privacy** ⭐ (Q53) | **Screening of individuals from others** (internal & external) |
| Roominess | Effect of maximum benefit from minimum space (proportions 🎯 L:B ≈ 1.2–1.5) |
| Grouping | Related rooms adjacent (kitchen near dining) 🎯 |
| Circulation | Horizontal (passages) & vertical (stairs) movement; ⭐ horizontal circulation ≈ 20–25% of plan area (asked in Estimation bank) |
| Flexibility | Rooms usable for multiple purposes 🎯 |
| Sanitation | Light, ventilation, cleanliness, sanitary units 🎯 |

## 1.2 Room / Area Requirements — ⭐ NUMBER TABLE (memorize!)

## 1.3 Ventilation & Services — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Ventilators: total area = 5% of floor area** (Q19) — in addition to doors & windows.
- Windows/openings for light+ventilation ≥ 1/10th (10%) of floor area (dry hot climate ~10%, humid ~15–20%) 🎯.
- ⭐ **Horizontal distance between building's accessible parts and electric supply mains = 1.2 m–2.0 m** (Q61) (vertical clearance low/medium voltage: 2.5 m 🎯).
- Q54 (X, garbled) topic = fresh-air/ventilation rate per person (m³/h) — standard ~20–30 m³/h/person for odorous environments 🎯.

## 1.4 Site Coverage, Setbacks & Height — ⭐ Kerala-rule numbers

| Rule | PSC key |
|------|---------|
| **Permissible covered area, plot 210–1000 m²** | **40% of site area** ⭐ (Q46) |
| **Rear setback** | **2.0 m** ⭐ (Q60) |
| **Front open space (building height <10 m)** | **minimum 3 m** ⭐ (Q64) |
| **Street width ≥ 12 m → max building height** | **24 m** ⭐ (Q65) — general rule: height ≤ 2× (street width + front open space) 🎯 |
| Side setback (typical KMBR residential) | 1.0–1.2 m 🎯 |

- **FSI/FAR = Floor Space Index / Floor Area Ratio = total covered area of all floors ÷ plot area** ⭐ (Q40 was corrupt but FSI IS the answer PSC wanted — know the definition cold; KMBR caps FAR ~3 for residential without premium 🎯).
- Plinth height: min 45–60 cm above ground/road level 🎯.
- Set-back line / building line vs street line: building line = line up to which building may lawfully extend 🎯.

## 1.6 Plans & Drawings — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Service plan:** drawn to SAME scale as building plan; includes **plans & sections of private water supply and sewage disposal systems** (Q41).
- Site plan: plot boundaries, abutting streets, north line — ⭐ **NBC: site plan scale not less than 1:500** (Estimation bank Q63 PSC key) — cross-subject repeat!
- Key/location plan: position of plot w.r.t. neighbourhood, scale ≥ 1:10,000 🎯.
- Building plan scale: 1:100 typical 🎯.

## 1.7 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- FAR numerical: plot 500 m², total floor area 750 m² → FAR = 1.5.
- Carpet area vs plinth area vs floor area (carpet ≈ 50–60% plinth) — overlaps Estimation.
- Minimum door size residential (0.9×2.0 m), window sill height (0.9 m), riser/tread rules (stairs file).
- Kerala-specific: KMBR vs KPBR (Panchayat) jurisdiction, coastal CRZ setbacks.
- Occupancy load (persons/m²) for assembly vs office.
