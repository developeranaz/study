## 6.2 Door & Window Types 🎯 (expect matching Qs)

- Doors: battened & ledged (+braced, +framed), framed & panelled (most common), glazed, flush (solid/hollow core — modern), louvred, revolving (public buildings, air-lock), sliding, swing (double-action), collapsible & rolling steel (security/shops/godowns).
- Windows: casement, sash/glazed, louvred, bay (projecting), clerestory (above adjacent roof — light+ventilation), dormer (on sloping roof), gable, lantern (over flat roof), corner, skylight.
- ⭐ **Fire-resistant construction: minimum timber door LEAF thickness = 4 cm** (Q4).
- Standard sizes 🎯: external door 1.0×2.1 m; internal 0.9×2.1 m; bathroom 0.75×2.1 m. Window width rule: ⅛ of room perimeter (thumb).

## 6.3 Lintels — ⭐ 3 number facts asked

- Lintel = horizontal member over opening carrying wall load (bending member — unlike arch).
- ⭐ **Depth of lintel = 15 cm** (Q28) (thumb: span/12, min 15 cm; width = wall thickness).
- ⭐ **RCC lintel concrete mix = 1:2:4** (Q11).
- ⭐ **Bar diameter for lintel span 1.5–2 m = 12 mm** (Q29) (main bars; ≤1 m: 10 mm; 6 mm stirrups/hangers 🎯).
- Bearing of lintel: 10–20 cm or equal to depth (whichever more; thumb: 10 cm min, or span/10) 🎯 likely.
- Types: timber (oldest, fire-risk), stone (weak in tension, ≤1 m), brick (≤1 m light loads), RCC (universal — any span, precast/cast-in-situ), steel (channel/I-sections when depth restricted), reinforced brick (RB) 🎯.
- Loading on lintel: triangular load within equilateral triangle above opening (arch action) 🎯 AE-level.
- Sunshade/chajja often combined with RCC lintel — projection 45–60 cm 🎯.

## 6.4 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- "Horizontal member dividing window" → transom; "vertical division" → mullion (reverse of Q26/Q32 pattern).
- Flush door: modern, solid-core vs hollow-core.
- Revolving door use-case; collapsible gate (vertical channels + flats on rollers).
- Clerestory window definition (Kerala PSC favourite in older papers).
- Bearing/effective span of lintel; window area thumb rules (links to bye-laws 5%/10% facts).
