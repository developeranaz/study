# 03 — Volumes & Surface Areas

*8 of 53 Qs. Cone/sphere/cube/cylinder — formulas plus two clean numericals.*

## 3.1 Formula Sheet — ⭐ all asked

| Solid | Volume | Surface |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Cube (side a) | a³ | **total = 6a²** ⭐ (Q28); lateral 4a² 🎯 |
| Cylinder | πr²h | **curved = 2πrh** ⭐ (Q26); total 2πr(r+h) 🎯 |
| **Cone** | (1/3)πr²h = **(1/12)πd²h** ⭐ (Q14) | curved = πrl, l=√(r²+h²) 🎯 |
| **Sphere** | **(4/3)πr³** ⭐ (Q23) | 4πr² 🎯 |
| Hemisphere 🎯 | (2/3)πr³ | curved 2πr², total 3πr² |
| **Frustum (bucket)** | **(πh/3)(R²+Rr+r²)** ⭐ (Q20 — options only offered hollow-cylinder forms → keyed "None of these") | |

⚠️ TRAP (Q14): (1/12)πd²h IS the cone formula in diameter form — substitute r = d/2 to see it.
⚠️ TRAP (Q20): πh(R²−r²) = HOLLOW CYLINDER, not bucket. Bucket = frustum.

## 3.2 🧮 Worked PSC Numericals

1. Q25: cone, base DIAMETER 20 cm (r=10), h=15 → V = (1/3)π×100×15 = 500π = **1570 cm³**. ⚠️ diameter given, not radius.
2. Q30: sphere surface = 36π → 4πr² = 36π → r = 3 → V = (4/3)π×27 = **36π cm³** (surface number = volume number at r=3, like the r=3 coincidence).
3. Q31: cube side 60 mm = 6 cm → V = **216 cm³**. ⚠️ unit switch mm→cm is the whole trick.
