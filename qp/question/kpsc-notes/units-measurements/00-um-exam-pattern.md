# 00 — How KPSC Asks Units and Measurements

*Derived from forensic reading of all 53 UM questions (6.6% of paper).*

## The Big Surprise

**UM is really two papers stapled together**: (1) unit systems & conversion factors (~34%), and (2) school-level mensuration numericals (~40%). The mensuration Qs are the easiest calculations in the entire exam — 3-4-5 triangles, r=7 circles (÷22/7 friendly), perfect squares. If an answer isn't clean, recheck.

## Question Style

- Numericals designed for NO calculator: Pythagorean triples (3-4-5, scaled), π as 22/7 with r=7 multiples, perfect squares (625, 216).
- Conversion factors asked point-blank: 25.4 mm, 735.5 W, 10⁷ ergs, 2.47 acres, 7850 kg/m³.
- 7 X-questions — highest corruption rate of any subject (13%). But every X intent is recoverable and re-askable: kcal, rhombus d₁d₂/2, 24.7 acres, micro, 1 g/cm³, mean=20, π radians. All in notes.
- Hectare/are/acre cluster asked 3× (Q7, 8, 22 + X Q33) — guaranteed mark.

## Trap Catalogue

| Trap | Resolution |
|------|-----------|
| **Metric HP vs British HP** | Metric = **735.5 W** ⭐ (Q6); British/imperial = 746 W. 746 is the bait when stem says "metric". |
| **Hectare→acre direction** | 1 ha = **2.47 acres** ⭐ (Q22); 1 acre = 0.405 ha. Q33 (X) baited with 40.47 (that's ares!). |
| **Trapezium vs trapezoid vs rhomboid** | PSC key (Q11): trapezium = only two sides parallel, all four angles different. Rhombus = 4 equal sides; rhomboid = parallelogram, adjacent sides unequal. |
| **Steel unit weight** | Mild steel = **7850 kg/m³** ⭐ (Q34) — 7650 is the classic bait. RCC 2500, PCC 2400, water 1000. |
| **Segment vs sector** | Segment = arc + CHORD ⭐ (Q15); sector = arc + two RADII. (Same trap as surveying/ED.) |
| **Poise conversion** | 1 poise = **0.1 N·s/m²** ⭐ (Q50) (1 Pa·s = 10 poise). |
| **Pascal** | 1 Pa = 1 **N/m²** ⭐ (Q51) — N/mm² = MPa is the bait. |
| Cone volume in d-form | (1/3)πr²h = **(1/12)πd²h** ⭐ (Q14) — recognise the d² disguise. |
| Bucket volume | Bucket = FRUSTUM: (πh/3)(R²+Rr+r²). Q20 options offered hollow-cylinder πh(R²−r²) → keyed "None of these" ⭐. |
| −40° crossover | **−40°C = −40°F** ⭐ (Q49) — the only temperature where both scales meet. |
