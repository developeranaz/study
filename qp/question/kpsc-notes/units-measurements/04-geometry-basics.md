# 04 — Geometry Basics (Figures, Angles, Circle Parts)

*5 of 53 Qs. Definitions and one-step angle logic.*

## 4.1 Quadrilateral Family — ⭐ asked 2×

- ⭐ **Triangle is NOT a quadrilateral** (Q29 — quadrilateral = 4 sides).
- ⭐ **Trapezium: only TWO sides parallel, four angles different** (Q11 PSC key).
- ⚠️ TRAP ladder (know all four):
  - Trapezium — exactly one pair of parallel sides.
  - Trapezoid — NO parallel sides (Indian/British convention; US swaps the two!). PSC follows Indian: keyed trapezium in Q11.
  - Rhombus — all 4 sides equal, opposite sides parallel.
  - Rhomboid — parallelogram with adjacent sides unequal, no right angles.

## 4.2 Triangle Types — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **One angle = sum of other two → RIGHT-angled triangle** (Q35).
  🧮 A = B + C and A+B+C = 180 → A = 90°.
- 🎯 Equilateral 60-60-60; isosceles 2 equal sides; scalene all different; obtuse >90°.
- 🎯 Exterior angle = sum of two interior opposite angles.

## 4.3 Angles at a Point / Line — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Two lines meeting at a point: interior + exterior angles total = 360°** (Q42).
- 🎯 Angles on a straight line = 180°; vertically opposite angles equal; complementary = 90°, supplementary = 180°.

## 4.4 Circle Parts — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Segment = region between arc and its CHORD** (Q15); sector = between arc and two RADII ⚠️ (recurring PSC trap — also in ED and Surveying banks).
- 🎯 Chord, diameter (longest chord), tangent (touches at one point), secant (cuts at two).
- 🎯 Angle in semicircle = 90°; angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference.

## 4.5 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- Sum of interior angles of polygon = (n−2)×180°; quadrilateral = 360°.
- "Cyclic quadrilateral opposite angles" → sum 180°.
- "Number of diagonals of hexagon" → n(n−3)/2 = 9.
- Parallelogram properties: diagonals bisect each other (rhombus: at 90°).
