## 5.1 🧮 Worked PSC Numericals

1. ⭐ Q38: 37.698 ÷ 1000 = **0.037698** (shift decimal 3 left — count the zeros).
2. ⭐ Q45: train 80 m crosses bridge 120 m → distance = train + bridge = **200 m**.
   ⚠️ TRAP: crossing distance = LENGTH OF TRAIN + LENGTH OF BRIDGE, not bridge alone.
3. ⭐ Q46: x+y = 32, x−y = 10 → x = (32+10)/2 = **21**.
   Template: x = (sum+diff)/2, y = (sum−diff)/2.
4. ⭐ Q48: ₹100 increased 10% TWICE → 100 × 1.1 × 1.1 = **121**.
   ⚠️ TRAP: successive % is compound, not 100+20 = 120. Shortcut: a+b+ab/100 = 10+10+1 = 21%.
5. (Q47 X) intent: mean of 18, 22, 15, 32, 10, 23, 20 = 140/7 = **20** (not in options).
   🎯 Mean = Σx/n; median = middle of sorted; mode = most frequent.

## 5.2 Templates & Shortcuts

- Successive percentages: net % = a + b + ab/100 (discounts: negative).
- Train problems: crossing a POLE → distance = train length; crossing bridge/platform → sum of lengths; time = distance ÷ speed (km/h ÷ 3.6 = m/s 🎯).
- Two-equation sum/difference pairs → add and halve.
- Decimal ÷ 10ⁿ → move point n places left.

## 5.3 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- "Speed of train crossing platform in t seconds" (add 3.6 conversion).
- Simple vs compound interest for 2 years (difference = PR²/100²).
- Average of first n natural numbers = (n+1)/2.
- "Two numbers whose sum is S and product P" quadratic-type.
- Percentage increase then decrease by same % → net DECREASE (a²/100).
