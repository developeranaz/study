# 01 — Unit Systems & Conversion Factors

*18 of 53 Qs — THE biggest UM topic. Pure recall of factors.*

## 1.1 Unit Systems — ⭐ asked 2×

- ⭐ **MKS: M=Metre, K=KILOGRAM, S=Second** (Q1 — K is kilogram, NOT Kelvin ⚠️).
- 🎯 CGS = centimetre-gram-second; FPS = foot-pound-second; SI = 7 base units (m, kg, s, A, K, mol, cd).
- ⭐ **SI unit of power = WATT** (Q3) (energy/work = joule; force = newton).
- (Q4 X) intent: **unit of heat in MKS = kilocalorie (kcal)** 🎯; SI = joule; CGS = calorie/erg.

## 1.2 Length & Area Conversions — ⭐ asked 6×

| Conversion | Value |

## 1.3 Energy, Power, Pressure, Viscosity — ⭐ asked 5×

- ⭐ **1 joule = 10⁷ ergs** (Q5).
- ⭐ **1 metric HP = 735.5 W** (Q6) ⚠️ British HP = 746 W — stem said metric.
- ⭐ **1 pascal = 1 N/m²** (Q51) (1 N/mm² = 1 MPa 🎯; 1 bar = 10⁵ Pa 🎯).
- ⭐ **1 poise = 0.1 N·s/m²** (Q50) (CGS unit; 1 Pa·s = 10 poise; kinematic: 1 stoke = 10⁻⁴ m²/s 🎯).
- (Q37 X) intent: **10⁻⁶ prefix = micro (µ)** 🎯 — ladder: milli 10⁻³, micro 10⁻⁶, nano 10⁻⁹, pico 10⁻¹²; kilo 10³, mega 10⁶, giga 10⁹.

## 1.4 Density & Unit Weights — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Mild steel ≈ 7850 kg/m³** (Q34) ⚠️ bait 7650.
- (Q41 X) intent: **water = 1 g/cm³** = 1000 kg/m³ = 9.81 kN/m³ 🎯.
- 🎯 Ladder: RCC 2500, PCC 2400, brick masonry 1920, cement 1440 kg/m³.

## 1.5 Temperature — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **−40°C = −40°F** (Q49) — unique crossover point. 🧮 F = 1.8C + 32.
- 🎯 0°C = 32°F = 273 K; 100°C = 212°F.

## 1.6 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- "1 kWh = ? joules" → 3.6 × 10⁶ J.
- "1 newton = ? dynes" → 10⁵.
- "1 kg force = ? N" → 9.81.
- "1 gallon = ? litres" → 4.546 (imperial).
- "1 mile = ? km" → 1.609.
- "Unit of frequency / capacitance / luminous intensity" → hertz / farad / candela.
