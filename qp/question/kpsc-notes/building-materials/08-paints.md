# 08 — Paints

*4 of 79 Qs. One concept carries all four: know each INGREDIENT and its function.*

## 8.1 Ingredients & Functions — ⭐ the whole game

| Ingredient | Function | Common example |
|-----------|----------|----------------|
| **Base** | Body of paint, hides surface | **WHITE LEAD** ⭐ (Q15 — most common base); others: red lead, zinc oxide (zinc white), titanium white, aluminium powder |
| **Vehicle (binder)** | Liquid that holds base, spreads it, binds film | **LINSEED OIL** ⭐🔁 (Q21 most common vehicle; Q39 "linseed oil is used as VEHICLE") |
| Pigment | Gives colour | lamp black, ochre |
| Drier | Accelerates drying (absorbs O₂) | litharge, red lead, manganese dioxide |
| Thinner (solvent) | Reduces viscosity, evaporates | turpentine, naphtha |

- ⚠️ TRAP: base vs vehicle asked from BOTH directions (Q15, Q21, Q39). White lead = BASE (solid), linseed oil = VEHICLE (liquid). Turpentine = THINNER — appeared as distractor for base.
- 🎯 Next questions from this table: "litharge is a ___" → drier; "turpentine is a ___" → thinner; "function of a drier"; "boiled vs raw linseed oil" (boiled dries faster).

## 8.2 Paint Types — ⭐ Q51

- ⭐ **Plastic paint (plastic emulsion) → showrooms, auditoriums** (Q51) — attractive, quick-drying, washable.
- 🎯 Match-list candidates: luminous paint (glows — signboards), aluminium paint (radiators, gas tanks, resists heat/corrosion), anticorrosive paint (steel), cellulose paint (cars/aircraft), enamel (hard glossy, sanitary), cement paint (external walls, e.g. Snowcem), bituminous paint (underwater ironwork), fire-resisting/asbestos paint.

## 8.3 🎯 High-Probability Neighbourhood

- Defects of painting: blistering (moisture), fading, flaking, chalking (powdery), grinning (background shows), saponification (alkali attack).
- Varnish = resin + solvent (no pigment); distemper = whiting + glue + water (cheap interior); French polish = shellac in spirit.

## 8.4 Flash-list

White lead = base | linseed oil = vehicle | turpentine = thinner | litharge = drier | plastic paint = showrooms/auditoriums
