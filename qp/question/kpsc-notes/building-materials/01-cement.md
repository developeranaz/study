| Oxide | Typical % | Asked? |
|-------|----------:|--------|
| Lime (CaO) | 60–67 (≈64) | 🎯 (64 was a distractor in Q41) |

## 1.2 Bogue Compounds 🎯 (X-questions Q45/Q46 hint these WILL be asked cleanly)

| Compound | Formula | Role |
|----------|---------|------|
| Tricalcium silicate | C₃S (≈40%) | **Early strength (first 7 days) + initial setting** — Q45 statement "initial setting almost entirely due to C₃S" |
| Dicalcium silicate | C₂S (≈32%) | Later/ultimate strength (after 7 days) |
| Tricalcium aluminate | C₃A (≈10%) | First to react, flash set, vulnerable to sulphates |
| Tetracalcium aluminoferrite | C₄AF (≈8%) | Least contribution to strength |

- 🎯 **Low-heat cement**: LOW C₃S & C₃A, HIGH C₂S; C₃A below ~5% (Q46 fragment). Used in mass concrete/dams.
- 🎯 **Sulphate-resisting cement**: C₃A < 5%. **Marine constructions** use sulphate-resisting / Portland slag cement (Q54 was this question with corrupted options).

## 1.4 Special Cements

- ⭐ **Rapid hardening cement: strength at 3 days = OPC at 7 days** (Q3). ⚠️ TRAP option "3 days". (Reason: higher C₃S + finer grinding. Setting TIME is same as OPC — only hardening is rapid 🎯 classic trap.)
- ⭐ IS codes (memorize the two asked):
  - **Super sulphated cement — IS 6909**-1990 (Q8)
  - **Oil-well cement — IS 8229**-1986 (Q17)
- 🎯 Neighbourhood: OPC 33 grade = IS 269; 43 grade = IS 8112; 53 grade = IS 12269; PPC = IS 1489; RHC = IS 8041; low-heat = IS 12600; sulphate-resisting = IS 12330; white cement = IS 8042.
- 🎯 Other special cements PSC style: high-alumina (refractory, quick hardening), expansive (grouting), hydrophobic (wet climates/storage), coloured (white cement + pigment).

## 1.5 X-question topics to expect clean 🎯

- Q45: "OPC attains ~70% strength in 28 days…" statement pair on C₃S/C₂S strength timeline. Standard facts: ~1-day 16%, 3-day 40%, 7-day 65%, 14-day 90%, 28-day 99% of design strength.
- Q46: low-heat / sulphate-resisting composition limits (C₃A < 5%).
- Q54: cement for marine works; the corrupted options (lignin, sugars, polycarboxylates) are **admixtures**: lignin & polycarboxylates = plasticizers/water-reducers; sugar = retarder 🎯.
