# 06 — Lime & Gypsum

*8 of 79 Qs. Lime classification by clay % is the anchor — three Qs hang off it.*

## 6.1 Classification by Clay Content — ⭐ core table

| Lime | Clay % | Character |
|------|-------:|-----------|
| **Fat lime** (rich/pure/white/quick) | < 5% | Slakes vigorously, hardens ONLY with CO₂ (needs air), high plasticity |
| Feebly hydraulic | 5–10% | mild hydraulicity |
| Moderately hydraulic | 10–20% | |
| **Eminently hydraulic** | 20–30% | Nearly cement-like, sets under water |
| **Poor lime** | **> 30% clay** | ⭐ Q10 — impure, poor paste |

- ⭐ **Lime with > 30% clay = POOR lime** (Q10). ⚠️ TRAP: "eminently hydraulic" is 20–30%; ABOVE 30% it becomes poor.
- ⭐ **Lime from stones with high aluminium silicate (clayey impurities) = eminently HYDRAULIC lime** (Q42). Kankar lime = from kankar nodules (also hydraulic) — distractor.

## 6.2 Fat vs Hydraulic — ⭐ asked repeatedly

| Property | Fat lime | Hydraulic lime |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| Slaking | Vigorous, MORE heat | Slow, **LESS heat** ⭐ (Q56) |
| Setting | Needs CO₂/air | **Sets without air — in thick walls / under water** ⭐ (Q19: this property = **HYDRAULICITY**) |
| Damp situations | ✗ | ⭐ **Hydraulic lime used in damp situations** (Q33) |
| White washing / plastering | ⭐ **FAT lime** (Q48) | colour not pure white |

- ⭐ **Manufacture of fat lime = 2 operations** (Q49): (1) calcination of limestone → quicklime, (2) slaking → hydrated lime. ⚠️ don't add "grinding" — PSC counts 2.
- 🎯 Chemistry: CaCO₃ →(≈900 °C) CaO + CO₂ (calcination); CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ + heat (slaking); Ca(OH)₂ + CO₂ → CaCO₃ (carbonation/hardening).
- 🎯 IS 712 lime classes: A (eminently hydraulic — structural), B (semi-hydraulic — masonry mortar), C (fat — finishing/white wash), D (magnesium), E (kankar), F (siliceous dolomitic).

## 6.3 Gypsum & Plaster of Paris

- ⭐ **Plaster of Paris obtained by CALCINATION of GYPSUM** (Q31) — CaSO₄·2H₂O heated to ~160–170 °C → CaSO₄·½H₂O. ⚠️ distractors bauxite/limestone/dolomite.
- 🎯 PoP: very quick setting (5–10 min), used for ornamental work, false ceilings, wall punning; gypsum also = cement retarder (link to file 01).
