# 05 — Timber

*10 of 79 Qs. Two clusters: tree structure/defects and seasoning/moisture. Plywood is a reliable side-question.*

## 5.1 Tree Structure — ⭐ asked

- ⭐ **Growth in living trees is due to CAMBIUM layer** (Q73) — thin layer between sapwood and inner bark that forms new annual rings. ⚠️ distractors pith/heartwood/sapwood: pith = innermost (dead centre), heartwood = dead inner wood giving STRENGTH/timber, sapwood = outer living wood carrying SAP.
- ⭐ Q62 (X but answer inferable): **annual rings = combination of spring wood (early) + autumn wood (late)** — one ring per year, used to estimate tree age 🎯. (If PSC's intended options were heartwood/sapwood-based, the standard textbook answer remains spring+autumn wood.)
- 🎯 Medullary rays: radial, carry sap horizontally, bind annual rings together.

## 5.2 Defects — ⭐ two asked, know the whole family

| Defect | Cause / description | Q |
|--------|--------------------|---|
| **Knot** | Base of a removed branch — dark, hard ring | ⭐ Q74 |
| **Upsets** | **Fibres crushed transversely during growth by strong winds** | ⭐ Q76 |
| **Honey-combing** | **SEASONING defect** — internal cracks from drying stresses | ⭐ Q34 ⚠️ TRAP: knot/rind gall/foxiness offered — those are growth/decay defects |
| Rind gall | Curved swelling where a branch was improperly cut | distractor 2× |
| Foxiness | Red/yellow tinge — sign of decay begun | distractor |
| Shakes | Cracks/partial separation: star, cup, heart, ring | 🎯 |
| Twisted fibres / wandering heart | Twisting by wind | distractor |

- 🎯 Defect categories: due to natural forces (knots, shakes, upsets, twisted fibres), due to seasoning (bow, cup, twist, warp, split, check, honey-combing, case-hardening, collapse), due to fungi (dry rot, wet rot, blue stain), due to insects (termites, beetles, marine borers), due to conversion.

## 5.3 Moisture Content & Seasoning — ⭐ numbers

| Condition | Moisture | Q |
|-----------|----------|---|
| **Well-seasoned timber** | **10–12%** | ⭐ Q5 |
| **Timber for building frames** | **12–18%** | ⭐ Q28 |
| Air/natural seasoning brings moisture to | 10–20% | ⭐ Q20 statements |

- ⭐ Q20 (advantages of NATURAL/air seasoning): TRUE — needs no skilled supervision; cheap and simple. The "very slow process" statement is a DISADVANTAGE; "10–20% moisture" is a result. PSC answer: i and ii only.
- 🎯 Kiln seasoning: fast, controlled, costlier, needs skill; other methods: water seasoning, boiling, chemical/salt, electrical (Boucherie is PRESERVATION, not seasoning ⚠️).
- 🎯 Preservation: creosote, ASCU, coal tar; Boucherie process = preservative forced through green sap.

## 5.4 Plywood & Veneers — ⭐ both asked

- ⭐ **Plywood = assembled product of VENEERS + ADHESIVES** (Q35). Always ODD number of plies (3, 5, 7…), grains of adjacent plies at right angles 🎯.
- ⭐ **Max thickness of commercial veneer = 4 mm** per IS 14315:1995 (Q47).
- 🎯 Related boards: fibre board, particle board, block board (core strips 25 mm), laminboard, hardboard.
