# 03 — Concrete, Mortar & Aggregates

*12 of 79 Qs. Overlaps with Construction Technology — facts here also pay off there.*

## 3.1 Basics — ⭐ all asked

- ⭐ **Concrete without reinforcement = P.C.C.** (plain cement concrete) (Q16).
- ⭐ **Full strength of concrete achieved after 28 days** per IS (Q55). 🎯 strength-gain: 7-day ≈ 65%, 14-day ≈ 90% of 28-day.
- ⭐ **IS 456-1978 designates concrete in 7 grades** (M10–M40: M10, M15, M20, M25, M30, M35, M40) (Q58). ⚠️ current IS 456-2000 has more grades (up to M80) — PSC's question said 1978, answer **7**.
- 🎯 M20 → 20 N/mm² characteristic cube strength at 28 days (150 mm cube).
- Q66 (X) hints: **standard curing temperature = 27 ± 2 °C** 🎯.

## 3.2 Water-Cement Ratio — ⭐ Q13

- ⭐ **Lower w/c ratio → GREATER strength, LESSER workability** (Q13). Abrams' law: strength inversely proportional to w/c. ⚠️ TRAP: options pair strength & workability moving together — they move OPPOSITE.
- 🎯 Typical w/c 0.45–0.60; water for hydration alone ≈ 0.23 (23%).

## 3.3 Workability / Slump — 🔁 asked twice, values differ by source ⚠️

| Work | PSC-given slump |
|------|-----------------|
| Road construction / pavements | **25–50 mm** ⭐ (Q22) |
| Normal RCC work | **80–150 mm** ⭐ (Q67) |

- ⚠️ TRAP: textbooks vary (some give RCC 50–100 mm). Use the PSC-given values above when the same options reappear.
- 🎯 Neighbourhood: slump test apparatus = frustum 300 h × 200 base × 100 top mm; mass concrete 25–50 mm; compaction factor for very low workability; vee-bee for very stiff mixes.

## 3.4 Aggregates & Sand

- 🔁 **Max size of coarse aggregate for RCC = 20 mm** — asked twice verbatim (Q7, Q29) ⭐. (General rule: should not exceed ¼ of minimum member dimension, nor spacing limits.)
- ⭐ Fine vs coarse boundary: **4.75 mm sieve** 🎯 (was a distractor in Q65 — know it).
- ⭐ **Coarse sand for masonry passes ~3.175 mm (1/8") screen** (Q65).
- ⭐ Old BIS sieve convention (Q11): well-graded sand **passes BIS No. 480 and is retained on BIS No. 15** sieve — obscure legacy fact, memorize as-is: answer **15**.
- 🎯 Bulking of sand: max at 4–6% moisture (~25–40% volume increase), fine sand bulks more; disappears when saturated.

## 3.5 Mortar

- ⭐ **Heavy mortar: bulk density > 15 kN/m³** (Q50); light mortar < 15 kN/m³. ⚠️ absurd distractors (50/500) — anchor on 15.
- 🎯 Mortar proportions PSC style: masonry 1:6, plaster 1:4, rich/waterproof 1:3, pointing 1:1–1:2.

## 3.6 Admixtures 🎯 (from corrupt Q54 options)

- Plasticizers/water-reducers: **lignosulphonates (lignin), polycarboxylates**.
- Retarder: **sugar**, gypsum. Accelerator: calcium chloride. Air-entrainer: neutralized vinsol resin.
