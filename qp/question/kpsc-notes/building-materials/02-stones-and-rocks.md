# 02 — Stones & Rocks

*12 of 79 Qs. Rock classification is KPSC's favourite BM question — asked almost every paper.*

## 2.1 Geological Classification — ⭐ GUARANTEED QUESTION

| Class | Formed by | Examples |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Igneous** | Cooling of molten magma | granite, basalt, dolerite, trap |
| **Sedimentary** | Deposition in layers (aqueous) | **limestone** ⭐🔁 (Q2, Q40), **sandstone** ⭐ (Q4), shale, gypsum, laterite |
| **Metamorphic** | Change under heat/pressure | **marble** (from limestone) ⭐🔁 (Q37, Q53), slate (from shale), gneiss (from granite), quartzite (from sandstone) |

- 🔁 "Which is sedimentary?" asked 3× (Q2, Q4, Q40) — answer was **limestone/sandstone** every time; distractors always granite/basalt/dolerite (igneous) and slate/gneiss/marble (metamorphic).
- 🔁 "Marble is ___ rock" asked 2× (Q37, Q53) — **metamorphic**. ⚠️ TRAP distractor "foliated": marble is NON-foliated metamorphic; slate/gneiss/schist are foliated.
- ⭐ Rocks **deposited in layers, easily split along layers = sedimentary/stratified** (Q71). ⚠️ "aqueous rock" is a synonym distractor — PSC's accepted answer was **sedimentary**.
- 🎯 Memorize parent→metamorphic pairs: limestone→marble, shale→slate, granite→gneiss, sandstone→quartzite, mudstone→schist.

## 2.2 Igneous Sub-classification — ⭐ Q75 statement pair

- ⭐ **Eruptive/igneous rocks form by cooling of hot molten magma — TRUE** (Q75-I).
- ⭐ **"Trap is plutonic" — FALSE**: **trap & basalt = VOLCANIC** (cooled fast at surface, fine-grained); **granite = PLUTONIC** (cooled slow at depth, coarse-grained) (Q75-II). Hypabyssal (dolerite) = intermediate depth. 🎯 next question: "granite is ___" → plutonic.

## 2.3 Stone Properties & Uses

- ⭐ **Specific gravity of a good building stone ≥ 2.5** (Q23). 🎯 neighbourhood: water absorption ≤ 5% (24 hr), crushing strength ≥ 100 N/mm² for good stone.
- ⭐ **Hardest: DIAMOND** (Q25) — Mohs 10; talc = 1 (softest). ⚠️ options had "quartz" (Mohs 7) as bait.
- ⭐ **Roofing stone = SLATE** (Q36) — splits into thin sheets. 🎯 pairs: laterite = cheap walling (Kerala!), granite = piers/abutments, marble = ornamental/flooring, quartzite = concrete aggregate/rubble.
- ⭐ **Black marble → JAIPUR district** (Q32). ⚠️ TRAP: Jodhpur/Jabalpur/Jaisalmer sound-alikes. 🎯 Makrana (white marble, Taj Mahal) is the classic variation.
- ⭐ Disadvantage/advantage statements (Q18): TRUE about stones — dressing is tedious & time-consuming; RCC/steel alternatives are stronger. FALSE — "stones of desired quality easily available at moderate rate" (they are NOT). Correct pick = the false one when asked "incorrect statement".

## 2.4 🎯 High-Probability Variations

- Quarrying methods (blasting, wedging), dressing types, tests on stones (attrition/Deval, abrasion/Los Angeles, hardness/Dorry, durability/smith's test, crystallization).
- "Basalt & trap are ___ rocks" → volcanic igneous.
- Laterite questions (Kerala PSC loves local material): formed by weathering, contains iron oxide, soft when quarried & hardens on exposure.

## 2.5 Numbers flash-list

Sp. gr. ≥ 2.5 | diamond Mohs 10 / talc 1 | limestone = sedimentary | marble = metamorphic (Jaipur black) | slate = roofing | trap/basalt = volcanic, granite = plutonic
