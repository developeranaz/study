## 7.1 Carbon Content — ⭐ the classification ladder

| Material | Carbon % |
|----------|----------|
| Wrought iron | 0.02–0.15 (purest iron) |
| **Mild steel** | **0.10–0.25%** ⭐ (Q68 — PSC-given) |
| Medium carbon steel | 0.25–0.45 |
| High carbon (hard) steel | 0.45–1.5 |
| Cast iron | 2–4 (+ silicon) |

- ⚠️ TRAP: Q68 options were four overlapping ranges (0.25–0.40, 0.30–0.45…) — anchor on **0.10–0.25** for mild steel.
- ⭐ **Density of mild steel = 7850 kg/m³** (Q79). 🎯 = 78.5 kN/m³; E = 2×10⁵ N/mm².

## 7.2 Cast Iron Types — ⭐ Q69

- ⭐ **WHITE cast iron CANNOT be welded** (Q69) — carbon as cementite, very hard & brittle. Grey CI (graphite flakes, machinable), malleable CI (annealed white CI), nodular/ductile CI (spheroidal graphite) can be welded/worked.
- 🎯 Uses: grey CI → pipes, manhole covers; CI is strong in COMPRESSION, weak in tension ⚠️ classic.

## 7.3 Iron Ores & Pig Iron — ⭐ Q70

- ⭐ **Bessemer pig obtained from HAEMATITE ore** (Q70) (Fe₂O₃, ~70% iron, red).
- 🎯 Ore family: magnetite Fe₃O₄ (richest, ~72%, black), haematite (main ore), limonite (~60%, yellow-brown), iron pyrite FeS₂ ("fool's gold" — not used, sulphur harms).
- 🎯 Pig iron = first product of blast furnace; cast iron from pig iron (cupola); wrought iron by puddling; steel by Bessemer/open-hearth.

## 7.4 Alloys — ⭐ Q72

- ⭐ **Brass = COPPER + ZINC** (Q72). ⚠️ pair-trap: **Bronze = copper + TIN** 🎯 near-certain next question.
- 🎯 Others: duralumin (Al + Cu + Mg + Mn), solder (lead + tin), stainless steel (steel + chromium ≥ ~12% + nickel), gun metal (Cu+Sn+Zn).
