# 12 — Modern Surveying: Total Station, EDM, GPS, Remote Sensing

*KPSC weight: ~16 of 203 Qs (8%) and RISING in recent papers. Easy marks — pure fact recall.*

## 12.1 Total Station — ⭐ 7 direct questions

Total station = electronic theodolite + **EDM** + microprocessor (+ data collector).

- ⭐ **Fundamental measurements: horizontal angle, vertical angle and SLOPE DISTANCE** (Q48; Q135 — "EDM in a total station measures directly SLOPE distance").
- ⭐ Total station does **NOT directly measure LEVEL DIFFERENCE** (Q79) — elevation is computed from slope distance + vertical angle.
- ⭐ **Optical plummet → PRECISE CENTERING** (Q145, Q19 — replaces plumb bob; laser plummet in new models).
- ⭐ **Prism (corner-cube retro-reflector) reflects rays back precisely in the same direction** (Q128). Reflectorless TS works up to a few hundred m without prism 🎯.
- Instrument errors asked:
  - ⭐ **Tilting-axis error:** tilting (trunnion) axis not ⊥ vertical axis (Q20).
  - ⭐ **Slip:** instrument not firmly tightened on tripod head (Q22).
  - Line-of-sight (collimation) error: LOS not ⊥ tilting axis 🎯; vertical-index error: 0° not at zenith/horizontal 🎯.
- On-board software gives: coordinates (N, E, elevation), missing-line measurement, resection, stake-out/setting-out, area 🎯.

## 12.2 EDM (Electromagnetic Distance Measurement)

- ⭐ **Modern EDM uses INFRARED rays** (Q81) (+ visible laser & microwaves in some).
- Principle: phase difference (not travel time) of modulated wave; D = measured via phase shift + integer cycles.
- Types 🎯: Microwave (Tellurometer, long range, needs 2 operators), Visible light (Geodimeter), **Infrared (most common: short range ~1–3 km, one operator, prism reflector)**.
- Accuracy quoted as ±(a mm + b ppm) — constant + proportional parts. 🎯
- Distomat = compact EDM. 🎯

## 12.3 GPS — ⭐ 6 direct questions

- ⭐ **GPS = Global Positioning System** (asked 2× — Q129, Q134).
- ⭐ **Space segment: 24 satellites (21 active + 3 spares)** — PSC's Q130 answer **21**; constellation nominally 24, in **6 orbital planes**, 4 per plane, ~20,200 km altitude, 12-hr (11h58m) period, L1/L2 frequencies. ⚠️ If options have both 21 and 24: PSC accepted 21 for "available in space segment"; for "total satellites in GPS constellation" prefer 24.
- Three segments: Space / Control (master station Colorado + monitor stations) / User. 🎯
- Minimum satellites to fix 3-D position = **4** (3 for position + 1 for clock error) 🎯 near-certain future question.
- ⭐ **DGPS: differential GPS — needs at least 2 receivers** (Q46) (base at known point + rover; corrections improve accuracy to sub-metre).
- ⭐ **RTK = Real Time Kinematic** (Q47) — carrier-phase differential, cm accuracy, real-time corrections via radio link. ⭐ Q125: "modern GPS technology" = **Kinematic positioning technique**.
- Other GNSS 🎯: GLONASS (Russia), Galileo (EU), BeiDou (China), **NavIC/IRNSS (India — 7 satellites)** ⭐🎯 very likely in Kerala papers.

## 12.4 Remote Sensing & GIS

- ⭐ **Remote sensing = acquiring information about earth's surface WITHOUT physical contact** (Q176).
- Basis: electromagnetic radiation; sensors passive (sunlight) / active (radar, LiDAR). 🎯
- Platforms: ground, airborne, satellite (Indian: IRS series, Resourcesat, Cartosat 🎯).
- **GIS** = Geographic Information System — capture, storage, analysis of spatially referenced data; layers concept. 🎯
- Photogrammetry: measurements from photographs; aerial photos with **60% longitudinal & 30% side overlap** 🎯; parallax, stereoscopy. (Only fringe presence in bank, but cheap marks if asked.)
- LiDAR = laser-based ranging → DEM generation. 🎯

## 12.5 Quick-Fire Fact List (revision)

1. TS measures: H-angle, V-angle, slope distance.
2. TS cannot directly: level difference.
3. Optical plummet: precise centering.
4. Prism: retro-reflection.
5. EDM light: infrared.
6. GPS: 24 (21+3) satellites, 6 planes, 20,200 km, need 4 to fix.
7. DGPS: 2 receivers minimum.
8. RTK: Real Time Kinematic.
9. Remote sensing: no contact.
10. NavIC: India's system, 7 satellites.
