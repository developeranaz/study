# 07 — Theodolite & Traversing

*KPSC weight: ~12 of 203 Qs. One definition is asked FIVE times — free marks.*

## 7.1 The Five-Time Question

⭐🔁5× **"Theodolite is an instrument used for measuring very accurately ___"
→ HORIZONTAL AND VERTICAL ANGLES.** (Q26, 97, 108, 123 + variant)
⚠️ Distractors: "horizontal only", "vertical only", "linear measurements" — never fall for them.

## 7.2 Theodolite Anatomy & Facts

- Transit theodolite: telescope can be transited (revolved 180° in vertical plane about horizontal axis).
- ⭐ **Least count of (vernier) theodolite = 20 seconds** (Q67; common Indian vernier theodolite; micrometer ones read 1″).
- ⭐ **Fundamental lines/axes (Q44 answer: horizontal axis, line of collimation, axis of telescope):** full set = (1) vertical axis, (2) horizontal/trunnion axis, (3) line of collimation, (4) axis of altitude level, (5) axis of plate level.
  Relationships (permanent adjustments) 🎯:
  - Plate-level axis ⊥ vertical axis
  - **Line of collimation ⊥ trunnion (horizontal) axis** ⭐ (Q144) and intersects it
  - Trunnion axis ⊥ vertical axis
- ⭐ **Vertical circle is attached to the TRUNNION (horizontal) axis / telescope** (Q162) — rotates with telescope.
- Upper plate = vernier plate; lower plate = scale plate (graduated 0–360°). Upper clamp → both plates together (reading unchanged); lower clamp → lower plate. 🎯 classic future Q: "to measure angle, use upper/lower clamp when".

## 7.3 Telescope Motions — ⚠️ TRAP SET (asked 2×)

| Term | Motion |
|------|--------|
| **Transiting = Plunging = Reversing** | Revolving telescope 180° in **VERTICAL plane about horizontal axis** |
| **Swinging** | Rotating telescope in **HORIZONTAL plane about vertical axis** ⭐🔁2× (Q63, Q203) — right swing (clockwise) / left swing |

- **Face left / face right:** vertical circle to observer's left/right. ⭐ Q160: vertical circle LEFT + telescope target up = **"telescope normal"** (face left = normal). Changing face eliminates collimation & trunnion-axis errors; observing both faces + averaging removes most instrumental errors 🎯.
- Errors NOT eliminated by changing face: graduation errors (eliminated by measuring on different parts of circle / repetition) 🎯.
- Methods of measuring horizontal angles: (a) ordinary, (b) **repetition** (angle multiplied, precision ↑), (c) **reiteration/direction** (several angles from one station, closing the horizon) 🎯 likely.

## 7.4 Traverse Geometry — 🧮 formula bank (asked 5×)

| Quantity (closed traverse, n sides) | Sum |
|--------------------------------------|-----|
| **Interior angles** | **(2n − 4) × 90°** ⭐ (Q64) |
| **Exterior angles** | **(2n + 4) × 90°** ⭐ (Q8) |
| **Deflection angles** | **360°** ⭐🔁2× (Q21, Q152) |

- ⭐ **Deflection angle** = angle between prolongation of previous line and next line; value ranges **0° to 180°** ⭐🔁2× (Q151, Q153); Right (R/clockwise) or Left (L).
- 🧮 Q119: included angle 209°16′ → deflection = 209°16′ − 180° = **29°16′** ✓
  **Template: Deflection = |180° − included angle|.**

## 7.5 Latitude & Departure — ⭐ asked 2×

| Quantity | Formula | Direction |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| **Latitude** | **L = l·cos θ** ⭐🔁2× (Q65, Q82) | N (+) / S (−) |
| **Departure** | D = l·sin θ 🎯 | E (+) / W (−) |

(θ = reduced bearing.) Mnemonic: **"LAT-COS, DEP-SIN".**
- Consecutive coordinates = lat/dep of a line; independent coordinates = w.r.t. common origin. 🎯
- Closed traverse checks: ΣLatitude = 0 and ΣDeparture = 0.
- **Closing error** e = √((ΣL)² + (ΣD)²); direction tan δ = ΣD/ΣL. 🎯 very likely numerical.
- **Balancing methods** 🎯: Bowditch rule (correction ∝ length of line — for compass+chain equally precise), Transit rule (∝ latitude/departure — angles more precise), Graphical, Axis method.
- Gale's traverse table = tabular computation of a traverse 🎯.

## 7.6 Traversing Methods & Checks 🎯

- Open traverse checks: cut-off lines, auxiliary point observations.
- Included-angle method most accurate for closed traverse; deflection-angle method for open (route) traverses (roads/railways/canals).
- Angular error of closure permissible: least count × √n (ordinary theodolite work).
