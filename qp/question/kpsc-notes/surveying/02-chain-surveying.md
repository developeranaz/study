## 2.1 Chains — THE Number Table (🔁 asked repeatedly)

| Chain | Length | Links | Length/link |
|-------|--------|------:|-------------|
| **Metric chain** | 20 m | 100 | 0.2 m |
| **Metric chain** | 30 m | **150** ⭐🔁2× | 0.2 m |
| **Gunter's / Surveyor's chain** | **66 ft** ⭐ | 100 | 0.66 ft |
| **Engineer's chain** | **100 ft** ⭐ | 100 | 1 ft |
| **Revenue chain** | 33 ft | 16 | — 🎯 |

- ⭐ **Links per metre in metric chain = 5** (each link 20 cm).
- ⭐ Two consecutive links connected by **three rings** (oval rings; gives flexibility).
- Tallies at every 5 m (metric); brass rings at every 1 m; total length stamped on handle. 🎯
- Gunter's chain: 10 chains = 1 furlong, 80 chains = 1 mile; 10 sq chains = 1 acre. 🎯

## 2.3 Instruments for Chaining

- **Ranging rod:** length **2–3 m** ⭐, bands 20 cm, dia 30 mm; ranging poles 4–8 m. 🎯
- Arrows: 15 (10 asked elsewhere as 10? — standard: 10 arrows per chain, 4 mm dia, 40 cm long) 🎯.
- **Line ranger:** fixing **intermediate points on chain line** ⭐ (two prisms/mirrors; no ranging assistant needed).
- **Offset rod, plumb bob, pegs, whites.**
- **Optical square** (also in file 13): sets out **90°** ⭐🔁, mirrors at **45°** ⭐🔁3×, horizon glass **half silvered half unsilvered** ⭐, angle between first incident & last reflected ray = **twice** the mirror angle ⭐. Cross-staff = simpler 90° instrument. 🎯 Prism square: 45° prism, no adjustment needed.

## 2.4 Ranging

- **Direct ranging:** ends **mutually intervisible** ⭐🔁2×.
- **Indirect/Reciprocal ranging:** ends NOT intervisible (hill between / ends not visible) ⭐ — two chainmen at intermediate points m, n mutually direct each other.
- ⭐ Error due to **bad ranging = cumulative positive** (measured length always too long).
- ⭐ Stepping (measuring on slopes): easier **downhill**; difficult **uphill** (Q181 answer: uphill = more difficult).

## 2.5 Offsets

- **Perpendicular offsets** = measurements at right angles to chain line ⭐; oblique offsets when >90° difficulty.
- ⭐ Offset should not be longer than **15 m** (limit of tape length/accuracy — PSC answer).
- **Tie line:** joins two fixed points on main lines — purpose: **avoid long offsets** ⭐ AND take details; check line/proof line: **checks accuracy** of framework. ⚠️ TRAP: tie line's *primary* stated purpose in PSC answer = avoid long offsets from chain line.
- Long offsets → error due to direction more serious. Degree of accuracy of offset laying (limiting length): l = 0.025r√(s) style problems 🎯 (rare at JE level).

## 2.6 Chain/Tape Corrections — 🧮 GUARANTEED NUMERICAL

**Master formula:** True length = (L′ / L) × Measured length
where L = nominal chain length, L′ = actual (faulty) chain length.

- 🧮 Q32: 20 m chain, 10 cm too long, measured 250 m →
  True = (20.10/20) × 250 = **251.25 m** ✓
- 🧮 Q138: 20 m chain, 5 cm too long, measured 634.4 m →
  True = (20.05/20) × 634.4 = **635.99 m** ✓
- Chain **too long** → measured value too SMALL → true length LARGER (multiply by >1).
- Chain **too short** → true length smaller. ⚠️ Sign intuition is the trap.
- For **areas**: True area = (L′/L)² × measured area. 🎯 likely next variation.

**Tape corrections (for precise work) — 🎯 know the five:**

| Correction | Formula | Sign |
|------------|---------|------|
| Standardisation | Ca = L(l′−l)/l | ± |
| Slope | Csl = h²/2L | always − |
| Temperature | Ct = α(Tm−T0)L | ± |
| Pull/tension | Cp = (P−P0)L/AE | ± |
| Sag | Cs = W²L/24P² (w²l³/24P²) | always − |

- Sag & slope corrections are ALWAYS negative; error from sag = cumulative positive. 🎯

## 2.7 Errors in Chaining (links to file 11)

| Error source | Nature |
|--------------|--------|
| Bad ranging | **Cumulative +** ⭐ |
| Chain too long/short | Cumulative (systematic) |
| Sag | Cumulative + |
| Temperature/pull variation | Cumulative ± |
| Non-horizontality | Cumulative + |
| Careless holding/marking | Compensating |
| Wrong reading/booking | Mistake |

⚠️ Q109 asked "cumulative errors proportional to…" — answer given "None of these" (cumulative ∝ L; compensating ∝ √L — memorize this pair 🎯🔁 near-certain future question).

## 2.8 Chain Triangulation Framework Terms 🎯

Main stations, main survey lines, base line (longest, through centre), tie stations/subsidiary stations, check lines. Well-conditioned triangle: angles between **30° and 120°** (ideal ~60°, equilateral). 🎯 very likely.
