## 13.2 Optical Square (deep facts — 4 questions in bank)

- Two mirrors at **45°** (Q52, Q92) → deflects ray **90°** (Q25).
- ⭐ Angle between first incident ray and last reflected ray = **TWICE the mirror angle** (Q111).
- ⭐ Horizon glass: **half silvered, half unsilvered** (Q99); index glass fully silvered.
- Used for long offsets; tested by double reflection. Principle: double reflection (like sextant).
