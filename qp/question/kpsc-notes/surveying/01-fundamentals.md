# 01 — Fundamentals of Surveying

*KPSC weight: ~12 of 203 Qs directly, but concepts feed every other topic.*

## 1.1 Definition & Object

Surveying = art of determining **relative positions of points** on, above or beneath the earth's surface by measuring **horizontal distances, vertical distances (elevations) and angles/directions**.

- ⭐ PSC FACT: *"The art of determining and representing relative heights or elevations of different points"* = **Levelling** (a branch of surveying).
- Plan = large-scale, horizontal projection, small area (curvature ignored). **Map = small-scale representation** of large area. ⭐ PSC asked: graphical representation on small scale = **Map**.

## 1.2 Fundamental Principles — 🔁 asked 5×, GUARANTEED question

1. **Work from the WHOLE to the PART** — 🔁 5×.
   - Why? → **To prevent accumulation of errors** (⭐ asked directly). Errors get localized, not magnified.
   - Method: establish a system of control points with high precision first, then fill details with lower precision.
2. **Fix a new station by at least two measurements** (linear or angular) from fixed reference points.

⚠️ TRAP: distractor is always "part to the whole" — never pick it.

## 1.3 Classification of Surveying

**Primary classification:**

| Type | Curvature | Extent |
|------|-----------|--------|
| **Plane surveying** | Ignored (earth = flat) | Small areas |
| **Geodetic surveying** | Considered (earth = spheroid) | Large areas; done by Survey of India |

- ⚠️ TRAP (PSC contradicts itself): limit of plane surveying / when curvature is considered:
  - Q27 answer: **200 km²**; Q202 answer: **250 km²**; Q120 answer: **260 km²**.
  - Textbook (Punmia): ~**250 km²** (196 km² ≈ 12 miles arc ≈ chord difference 1 in 10⁶).
  - **Rule: pick 250 if available; otherwise the closest option to 250.**
- In geodetic survey, a line joining two points is a **curved line (arc)**; in plane survey it's straight. Spherical triangle angle sum exceeds 180° by **spherical excess (~1″ per 195 km²)** — 🎯 likely variation.

**Functional classifications (⭐ each asked):**

| Survey | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Topographical** | Natural features — valleys, rivers, lakes, hills |
| **Cadastral** | Property/legal boundaries, land records 🎯 |
| **City survey** | Town layout, water/sewer lines |
| **Geological** | **Different strata of earth's crust** ⭐ |
| **Mine survey** | Underground workings |
| **Hydrographic** | Water bodies, shorelines, soundings 🎯 |
| **Astronomical** | Absolute positions via heavenly bodies 🎯 |
| **Location survey** | Setting out works on ground |
| **Engineering survey** | Data for engineering design 🎯 |

- **GTS = Great Trigonometrical Survey** ⭐ (of India; baseline network by triangulation).
- **Reconnaissance:** preliminary walkover; surveyor prepares a **key plan** ⭐.

## 1.4 Scales & Representative Fraction (RF)

- RF = map distance / ground distance (same units, unitless fraction).
- 🧮 Solved pattern (🔁 2×): 1 cm on map = 10 m ground → RF = 1/(10×100) = **1/1000**.
  - Template: 1 cm = X m → RF = 1/(100X).
- Scale types: plain scale (2 units), diagonal scale (3 units — units, tenths, hundredths), vernier scale, scale of chords (angles) — 🎯 asked in Engineering Drawing too.
- ⭐ Smallest length distinguishable on map ≈ **0.2 mm (0.02 cm)**. (PSC's Q190 answer 0.2 cm is loose — the standard figure is 0.2 mm; on exam pick the "0.2" option.)
- 🧮 Q201 style (length of scale): Length of scale = RF × max distance to be measured.
  - 20 miles = 5 cm → RF = 5/(20×1.609×10⁵ cm) ≈ 1/643,600. Scale length for 5 km = (1/643600)×5×10⁵ cm ≈ **0.777 cm**.

## 1.6 Units & Misc Facts Asked

- ⭐ Sidereal day divided into **24 (sidereal) hours**.
- Sexagesimal system: 1 circumference = **360°**, 1° = 60′, 1′ = 60″. Centesimal: 400ᵍ (French). Hours system: 24ʰ. (Q169 was corrupt but topic is testable — 🎯 know all three systems.)
- ⭐ Average length of a **pace ≈ 80 cm**. Pacing/passometer/pedometer/odometer/speedometer = approximate linear measurement methods (accuracy order: pacing 1/50 to 1/100). 🎯 Passometer counts paces; odometer measures wheel revolutions.
- **Fathometer** = echo-sounding instrument, measures **depth of water/oceans** ⭐ (hydrographic surveying; 1 fathom = 6 ft = 1.83 m 🎯).
- **Mean Sea Level (MSL)**: average of **hourly** tide heights over **19 years** (Metonic cycle) ⭐⚠️ — one PSC answer accepted "20 years" (Q124), another "19" (Q172). Prefer **19**; else nearest. India's datum: MSL at **Mumbai (formerly Karachi)** — 🎯.
